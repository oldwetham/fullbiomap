Each sternothyroid pulls down the thyroid cartilage, which forms the Adam's apple. By lowering this part of the larynx, or voice box, the sternothyroid helps create different tones of voice. The sternothyroid also pulls the larynx down when a person swallows. Each sternothyroid runs from the sternum, or breastbone, to the thyroid cartilage at the front of the neck.
