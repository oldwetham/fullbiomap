Each ureter is a tube that carries urine from a kidney to the bladder. Each ureter runs down the back wall of the abdomen. Muscle in the ureter wall pushes urine along in waves called peristalsis. Each ureter enters the bladder wall at a slant, which stops urine from flowing back.
