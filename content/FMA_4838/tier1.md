The azygos vein carries blood from the back walls of the chest and abdomen toward the heart. It collects blood from veins of the chest wall, the esophagus and the lungs. It starts in the lower back and rises into the chest along the right side of the backbone. It empties into the superior vena cava, a large vein that returns blood to the heart from the upper body.
