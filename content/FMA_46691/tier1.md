Each hyoglossus muscle lowers and flattens the tongue. The hyoglossus also pulls the tongue back. Each hyoglossus runs from the hyoid bone, a U-shaped bone in the upper neck, to the side of the tongue. The hyoglossus is an extrinsic tongue muscle, which means it starts outside the tongue but attaches inside the tongue.
