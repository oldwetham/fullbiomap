The external iliac artery is the main source of blood for the leg. In the lower torso, the external iliac artery branches off the common iliac artery, which supplies the pelvis and leg. After crossing the body wall, the external iliac artery is renamed the femoral artery. Arteries can change names at landmarks like this, just as a street changes names at a crossing.
