The teres major extends the arm, moving the arm backward, and helps turn the arm inward. The teres major also helps adduct the arm, bringing the arm down to the side of the body. The teres major starts on the lower corner of the scapula, or shoulder blade. The thick, flat teres major lies below the teres minor, a muscle that turns the arm outward.
