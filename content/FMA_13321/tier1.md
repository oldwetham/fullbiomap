Each clavicle, or collarbone, holds the shoulder joint up and out from the body's trunk, so the arm can move freely. The clavicle also protects the nerves and blood vessels under it and passes forces from the arm to the trunk. The clavicle is an S-shaped bone at the front of the shoulder. The clavicle's inner end joins the sternum, the flat bone at the center of the chest.
