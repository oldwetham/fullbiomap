Rod cells let us see in dim light, such as at night. Rods and cones are the two types of photoreceptor, cells that respond to light. Rods sit in the outer edges of the retina, the light-sensing layer at the back of the eye. In dim light, cones cannot respond and rods cannot sense color, so everything looks gray.
