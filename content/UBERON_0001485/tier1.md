Each knee joint works mainly as a hinge, letting the leg bend and straighten. Three bones form each knee joint: the thighbone (femur), the shinbone (tibia) and the kneecap (patella). Between the femur and tibia sit two cartilage pads called menisci, which cushion the bones. Strong bands called ligaments span between the bones and support the knee, especially when it is straight.
