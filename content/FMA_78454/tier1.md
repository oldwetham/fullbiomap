The third ventricle holds cerebrospinal fluid, a clear liquid that cushions the brain and spinal cord. This ventricle is the space between the left and right sides of the diencephalon, the brain's central region. Fluid flows in from the lateral ventricles, and the third ventricle adds more. The fluid then flows through the cerebral aqueduct, a narrow channel, into the fourth ventricle.
