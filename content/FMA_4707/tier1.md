The great cardiac vein collects blood from the front of both ventricles, the heart's lower pumping chambers. This vein runs along the front of the heart, then curves around to the back. There, the great cardiac vein empties into the coronary sinus, a large vein that drains the heart. Many other veins flow into the great cardiac vein along its path.
