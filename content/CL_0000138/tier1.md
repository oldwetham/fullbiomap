A chondrocyte is a cartilage cell that makes the matrix, the material around the cells. Each chondrocyte sits in a small space in the matrix called a lacuna. Cartilage has no blood vessels, so nutrients must spread through the matrix to reach the chondrocytes. Long bones grow longer as chondrocytes divide and make more cartilage.
