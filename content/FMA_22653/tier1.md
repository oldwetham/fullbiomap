Working alone, each splenius capitis turns and tilts the head toward that muscle's side. Working together, the right and left muscles tilt the head backward. Each splenius capitis starts at the midline and runs up and out to the head. Each splenius capitis attaches to the mastoid process, a bony bump just behind the earlobe.
