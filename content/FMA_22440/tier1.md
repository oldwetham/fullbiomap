The pectineus moves the thigh toward the body's midline, a movement called adduction. The pectineus also bends the hip, a movement called flexion. Each pectineus runs from the pubic bone to the femur, or thigh bone. The pectineus lies within the femoral triangle, an area where the hip meets the leg.
