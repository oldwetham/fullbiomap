A keratinocyte makes keratin, a protein that gives skin, hair and nails their hardness and water resistance. Keratinocytes are found in the skin and in the lining of the mouth. There they form a strong barrier against damage, water loss and germs. In the skin, the outermost keratinocytes are dead and keep flaking away, replaced by cells from below.
