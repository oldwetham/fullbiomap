The trochlear nerve controls the superior oblique, a muscle that rotates the eye. This rotation helps the eyes focus on an object close to the face. The trochlear nerve is the fourth cranial nerve, one of twelve nerves connected to the brain. It and two other cranial nerves control the extraocular muscles, the six muscles that move the eye.
