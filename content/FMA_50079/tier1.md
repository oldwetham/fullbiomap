Each middle cerebral artery brings blood to the temporal and parietal lobes of the brain. The temporal lobe processes hearing and also has a role in speech, memory and emotion. The parietal lobe helps process touch and the sense of the body's position in space. The middle cerebral artery branches off the internal carotid artery, which supplies blood to the brain.
