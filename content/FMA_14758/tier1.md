The testicular arteries carry blood to the testes, the male reproductive organs. In the abdomen, each testicular artery branches off the aorta, the body's largest artery. Each testicular artery then leaves the body cavity to reach the testes. On the way, each artery forms part of the spermatic cord, a bundle of nerves and blood vessels.
