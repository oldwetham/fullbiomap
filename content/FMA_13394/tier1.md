Each scapula, or shoulder blade, helps anchor the arm to the body. The scapula joins the humerus, or arm bone, to form the shoulder joint. Muscles that move the shoulder and arm attach to the scapula. The scapula is a flat, triangular bone with a raised ridge across the back of the bone.
