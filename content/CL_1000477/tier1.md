Pacemaker cells are special heart muscle cells that control heartbeats and set the heart rate. These cells form the sinoatrial (SA) node, high in the right atrium, an upper chamber of the heart. Pacemaker cells fire electrical signals on their own, at set intervals. Each signal passes to other heart muscle cells, so the heart contracts in a coordinated way.
