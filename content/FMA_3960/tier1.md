Each internal thoracic artery supplies blood to the front of the chest wall and the breast. This artery also feeds the thymus, a gland whose hormones help the immune system develop. The pericardium, the sac that encloses the heart, also gets blood from this artery. Each internal thoracic artery branches off a subclavian artery, which feeds the arm and shoulder.
