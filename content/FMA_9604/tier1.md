Each adrenal gland makes hormones, substances carried by the blood, that help the body cope with stress and control blood pressure. The gland sits near the top of a kidney, with an outer cortex and an inner medulla. The cortex makes aldosterone, a hormone that helps the body keep sodium and fluid and raises blood pressure. In short-term stress, the medulla sends out its main hormone, epinephrine, which is also known as adrenaline.
