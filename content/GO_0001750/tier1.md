The outer segment is where a photoreceptor, a sensory cell in the eye, detects light. The outer segment holds opsins, light-sensitive proteins that change light into a signal. Rods, the photoreceptors for dim light, have outer segments with stacked discs that hold a light-sensitive pigment. But in cones, the photoreceptors for color vision, the pigments sit in folds of the cell membrane.
