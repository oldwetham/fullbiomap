Each common carotid artery carries blood to its own side of the head and neck. The left common carotid artery branches off the aorta, the largest artery in the body. But the right one branches off the brachiocephalic artery, which is found only on the right side. Each common carotid artery then splits into the internal and external carotid arteries.
