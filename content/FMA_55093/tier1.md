Each submandibular gland makes saliva and releases it into the mouth. The two submandibular glands sit beneath the mandible, the lower jaw bone. Their saliva contains amylase, a substance that breaks down starch. This saliva is also thickened with mucus, a slippery substance that keeps tissues moist.
