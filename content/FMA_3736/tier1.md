The ascending aorta sends blood to the heart itself through its only branches, the coronary arteries. It is the first part of the aorta, the largest artery in the body. It rises about 5 cm from the left ventricle, the main pumping chamber on the heart's lower left side. There the aorta turns and curves to the left, forming the aortic arch.
