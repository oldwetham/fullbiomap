Each ophthalmic artery brings blood to the eye. This artery branches off the internal carotid artery, which supplies blood to the brain. The ophthalmic artery divides into two groups of branches. One group feeds the eyeball and the eye muscles, and the other feeds the orbit, the eye's bony socket.
