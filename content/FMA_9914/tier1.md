A vertebra bears weight and protects the spinal cord. Each vertebra is one bone of the vertebral column, or spine, in the neck and back. The spinal cord runs down the back through an opening in each vertebra. Neighboring vertebrae are joined by intervertebral discs, pads that let the spine move.
