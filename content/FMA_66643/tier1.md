The pulmonary veins carry blood rich in oxygen from the lungs to the heart. Blood from the lungs flows through vessels that join together to form the pulmonary veins. Four pulmonary veins, two on each side, empty into the left atrium, an upper chamber of the heart. After birth, the pulmonary veins are the only veins that carry blood rich in oxygen.
