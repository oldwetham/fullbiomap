The celiac trunk supplies blood to the stomach, liver, gallbladder, pancreas and spleen. In the abdomen, the celiac trunk branches off the aorta, the body's largest artery. A trunk, like the celiac trunk, is a large vessel that gives rise to smaller vessels. The celiac trunk has three major branches.
