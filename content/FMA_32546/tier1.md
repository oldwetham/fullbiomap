Each infraspinatus rotates the arm laterally, turning the front of the arm away from the body's midline. This muscle starts on the scapula, the shoulder blade bone at the back of the shoulder. The muscle sits below the spine of the scapula, a long ridge across the scapula's back. The infraspinatus is one of four rotator cuff muscles, which provide the main support for the shoulder joint.
