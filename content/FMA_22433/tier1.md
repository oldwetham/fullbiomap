The vastus intermedius helps straighten the knee, a movement called knee extension. This muscle is one of the quadriceps, four muscles at the front of the thigh. The vastus intermedius lies under the rectus femoris, another quadriceps muscle. All four quadriceps muscles share one tendon, which attaches to the patella, or kneecap.
