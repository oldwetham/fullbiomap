Proximal tubule cells return most water and sodium, and nearly all glucose, to the blood. These box-shaped cells form the proximal tubule, which receives the kidney's filtered fluid. Each cell's surface facing the tube's hollow center bears microvilli, thin projections that add area for absorbing. These cells pump substances, so each cell holds many mitochondria, which make the cell's energy.
