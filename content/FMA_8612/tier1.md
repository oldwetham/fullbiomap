The pulmonary trunk carries blood low in oxygen toward the lungs. The pulmonary trunk is the only vessel leaving the right ventricle, the heart's lower right pumping chamber. A valve at the base of the pulmonary trunk keeps blood from flowing back into the right ventricle. At the top of the heart, the pulmonary trunk splits into two pulmonary arteries, one for each lung.
