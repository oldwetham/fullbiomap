Each abductor pollicis longus inserts into the first metacarpal, which means the muscle moves that bone. The first metacarpal is the palm bone on the thumb side of the hand. This muscle lies deep in the back of the forearm. The muscle starts on the radius and ulna, the bones of the forearm.
