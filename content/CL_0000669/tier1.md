Pericytes help regrow and repair smooth muscle, the muscle in hollow organs and around blood vessels. A pericyte is a stem cell, an unspecialized cell that can become specialized cells. Pericytes are found in capillaries, the smallest blood vessels. Pericytes are long cells that also wrap around arterioles, which are very small arteries.
