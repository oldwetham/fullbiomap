Costal cartilages join most ribs to the sternum, or breastbone, at the front of the chest. Each costal cartilage is attached to the front end of a rib. Costal cartilage is made of hyaline cartilage, a smooth tissue that is strong and flexible. The springiness of costal cartilage lets the rib cage expand when you breathe.
