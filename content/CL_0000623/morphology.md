# Natural killer cell: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Structure and job:** Its granules release perforin, which punches pores in an infected cell's membrane, and granzymes, protein-digesting enzymes that pass through those pores and trigger the cell's apoptosis.  
  “The granules of the NK cells release perforins and granzymes. A perforin is a protein that forms pores in the membranes of infected cells. A granzyme is a protein-digesting enzyme that enters the cell via the perforin pores and triggers apoptosis intracellularly.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 21.2 Barrier Defenses and the Innate Immune Response, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/21-2-barrier-defenses-and-the-innate-immune-response)
- **Structure and job:** Germline-encoded activating receptors let it kill many kinds of target cells spontaneously, without prior antigenic activation, and it also regulates immune responses by releasing cytokines and by direct contact with other cells.  
  “A lymphocyte that can spontaneously kill a variety of target cells without prior antigenic activation via germline encoded activation receptors and also regulate immune responses via cytokine release and direct contact with other cells.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000623 natural killer cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000623)
