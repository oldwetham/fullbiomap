A stem cell found in the basal layer of the epidermis, hair follicle, and sebaceous gland, characterised by its quiescent nature, high proliferative potential, and capacity for long-term self-renewal. This cell maintains epidermal homeostasis by producing transit-amplifying cells that differentiate to replenish the stratified squamous epithelium.
