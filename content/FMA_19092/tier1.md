Each iliococcygeus forms part of the levator ani, a muscle that holds up the pelvic organs. The other part is a muscle called the pubococcygeus. The levator ani is the main muscle of the pelvic floor, the muscle sheet at the base of the pelvis. By resisting pressure from the belly muscles, the levator ani also helps in passing stool and in childbirth.
