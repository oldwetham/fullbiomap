The soft palate rises when you swallow, helping keep food out of the nasal cavity, the space inside the nose. The soft palate is the fleshy back part of the palate, the roof of the mouth. Most of the soft palate is skeletal muscle, which needs a signal from the nervous system to contract. The uvula, a fleshy bead of tissue, hangs down from the middle of the soft palate's back edge.
