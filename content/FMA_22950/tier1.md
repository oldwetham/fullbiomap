Each ulnar vein is a vein, a blood vessel that carries blood toward the heart. The ulnar vein starts at the palmar venous arches, which drain blood from the hand and fingers. The ulnar vein runs up the forearm along the ulna, the bone on the pinky-finger side, beside the ulnar artery. The ulnar vein then joins the radial vein, another forearm vein, to form the brachial vein, a deep vein of the arm.
