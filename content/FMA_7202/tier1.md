The gallbladder stores, concentrates, and releases bile, a digestive juice made by the liver. This pear-shaped organ sits under the liver. The gallbladder's lining removes water, making bile up to 10 times more concentrated. A hormone from the small intestine makes the gallbladder contract and release bile.
