The brachial vein carries blood into the axillary vein, which returns blood from the arm to the heart. The brachial vein is a deep vein of the upper arm, lying farther from the body's surface. This vein forms where the radial and ulnar veins, which run along the forearm bones, join together. Between the shoulder and elbow, it runs beside the brachial artery, which supplies much of the upper arm.
