The common hepatic artery brings fresh, oxygen-rich blood to the liver. The liver gets blood from two sources, and this artery is one of them. This artery also supplies parts of the stomach, small intestine and gallbladder. The common hepatic artery branches off the celiac trunk, a major artery in the abdomen.
