Each sternohyoid pulls down the hyoid bone, a U-shaped bone that supports the tongue. The sternohyoid also pulls down the larynx, or voice box, during swallowing and speaking. Each sternohyoid runs from the sternum, or breastbone, to the hyoid bone at the front of the neck. The sternohyoid is one of the infrahyoid muscles, a group of neck muscles below the hyoid bone.
