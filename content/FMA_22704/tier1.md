The iliocostalis cervicis is one of the back and neck muscles that move the spine. This muscle is part of the erector spinae, the large back muscles that keep you upright. This muscle belongs to the iliocostalis group, the part of the erector spinae placed toward the side. Within that group, the iliocostalis cervicis is the one linked to the cervical region, the neck area of the spine.
