The seminal vesicle is one of two glands that together make about 60 percent of semen. Each seminal vesicle sits behind the bladder and above the prostate gland. During ejaculation, sperm mix with fluid from the seminal vesicle. This fluid is rich in fructose, a simple sugar that fuels sperm movement.
