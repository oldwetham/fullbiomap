# Type B pancreatic cell: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Structure and job:** Beta cells make up about three quarters of each pancreatic islet and produce insulin, releasing it when blood glucose is high.  
  “The beta cell produces the hormone insulin and makes up approximately 75 percent of each islet. Elevated blood glucose levels stimulate the release of insulin.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 17.9 The Endocrine Pancreas, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/17-9-the-endocrine-pancreas)
- **Structure and job:** Beta cells detect excess glucose in the bloodstream and respond by releasing insulin into the blood.  
  “specific endocrine cells in the pancreas detect excess glucose (the stimulus) in the bloodstream. These pancreatic beta cells respond to the increased level of blood glucose by releasing the hormone insulin into the bloodstream.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 1.5 Homeostasis, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/1-5-homeostasis)
- **Structure and job:** A calcium signal inside the beta cell triggers its release of insulin.  
  “For example, in the β-cells of the pancreas, Ca2+ signaling leads to the release of insulin” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 9.2 Propagation of the Signal, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/9-2-propagation-of-the-signal)
