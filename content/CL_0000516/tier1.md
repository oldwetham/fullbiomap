Perineuronal satellite cells give nerve cells nutrients and structural support. These satellite cells are found in ganglia, groups of nerve cells outside the brain and spinal cord. There, satellite cells surround the cell body, the main part of each nerve cell. This arrangement, as seen under a microscope, gave satellite cells their name.
