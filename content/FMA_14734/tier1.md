Each inferior phrenic artery brings blood to the underside of the diaphragm, the dome-shaped breathing muscle. These arteries come as a pair from the aorta, the body's largest artery. Each one has a counterpart, a superior phrenic artery, which feeds the top of the diaphragm instead.
