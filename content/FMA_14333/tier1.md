Each common iliac vein carries blood into the inferior vena cava, a large vein that returns blood to the heart from the lower body. Each common iliac vein forms at the rim of the pelvis, where two veins join. One, the external iliac vein, brings blood from the leg. The other, the internal iliac vein, brings blood from the organs of the pelvis.
