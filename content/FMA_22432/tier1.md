Each vastus medialis helps extend, or straighten, the knee and keep the knee stable. The vastus medialis is one of the four quadriceps muscles at the front of the thigh. Of these four, the vastus medialis lies on the medial, or inner, side of the thigh. This muscle reaches the medial side of the patella, or kneecap.
