The superior vena cava carries blood from the head, neck, arms and chest back to the heart. This blood is low in oxygen because the body's tissues have used much of the oxygen. The left and right brachiocephalic veins, which drain the upper chest, join to form the superior vena cava. The superior vena cava empties into the right atrium, an upper chamber of the heart that receives blood.
