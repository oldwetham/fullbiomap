## HPA images of cell types

A cell type HPA names in a tissue shows HPA's image of a protein HPA scores high there (reliability enhanced, else supported). A protein that HRA's ASCT+B tables list as a biomarker of that cell type comes first: a row's gene and protein biomarkers belong to its last cell type, and HGNC's record of each biomarker's HGNC id gives the Ensembl gene HPA uses; a row counts only where that record also carries one of the row's names for the biomarker. Among biomarkers, the one listed by the most ASCT+B tables comes first, then the order below.
