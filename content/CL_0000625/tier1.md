CD8-positive T cells are white blood cells that can become cytotoxic T cells, which kill infected cells directly. Cytotoxic T cells kill target cells by triggering apoptosis, a controlled form of cell death. T cells travel in the blood and in lymph, a watery fluid that bathes tissues, and live in the spleen and lymph nodes. T cells are called CD4-positive or CD8-positive depending on which of two surface molecules they carry.
