Each flexor hallucis longus bends the big toe, a movement called flexion. The flexor hallucis longus lies deep in the back of the lower leg. The flexor hallucis longus starts on the fibula, the thin outer bone of the leg. The muscle ends in a tendon, a strong band that attaches to the big toe.
