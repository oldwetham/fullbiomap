The extensor carpi radialis brevis extends the hand at the wrist, moving the hand backward. This muscle also abducts the hand, moving the hand away from the body's midline. The muscle runs from the humerus, the upper arm bone, along the back of the forearm. The muscle's other end attaches to the third metacarpal, a long bone of the palm.
