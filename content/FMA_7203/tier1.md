Each kidney filters the blood, removes wastes, and sends the wastes out in urine. It also helps control blood pressure and makes a hormone that drives red blood cell development. It sits beside the spine at the back of the abdomen, protected by muscle, fat, and ribs. Each kidney holds over a million nephrons, the working units that do the filtering and make urine.
