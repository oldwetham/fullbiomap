# Podocyte: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Shape:** other: not squamous; uniquely shaped cell extending finger-like arms (pedicels) that cover the glomerular capillaries  
  “Here, the cells are not squamous, but uniquely shaped cells (podocytes) extending finger-like arms (pedicels) to cover the glomerular capillaries” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 25.4 Microscopic Anatomy of the Kidney, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/25-4-microscopic-anatomy-of-the-kidney)
- **Surface:** foot processes  
  “A specialized kidney epithelial cell, contained within a glomerulus, that contains "feet" that interdigitate with the "feet" of other podocytes.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000653 podocyte, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000653)
- **Special structure:** filtration slits: small gaps left between the cell's interdigitating projections, forming a sieve  
  “These projections interdigitate to form filtration slits, leaving small gaps between the digits to form a sieve.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 25.4 Microscopic Anatomy of the Kidney, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/25-4-microscopic-anatomy-of-the-kidney)
- **Special structure:** fused basement membrane shared with the capillary endothelial cells the podocytes embrace (part of the filtration membrane)  
  “A filtration membrane is formed by the fused basement membranes of the podocytes and the capillary endothelial cells that they embrace.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 25.4 Microscopic Anatomy of the Kidney, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/25-4-microscopic-anatomy-of-the-kidney)
- **Structure and job:** The podocytes' interdigitating projections leave small gaps (filtration slits) that act as a sieve: as blood passes through the glomerulus, 10 to 20 percent of the plasma filters between these fingers into Bowman's capsule.  
  “These projections interdigitate to form filtration slits, leaving small gaps between the digits to form a sieve. As blood passes through the glomerulus, 10 to 20 percent of the plasma filters between these sieve-like fingers to be captured by Bowman’s capsule and funneled to the PCT.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 25.4 Microscopic Anatomy of the Kidney, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/25-4-microscopic-anatomy-of-the-kidney)
- **Structure and job:** Because cells and medium-to-large proteins cannot pass between the podocyte processes, the processes act as a barrier that holds them back during filtration.  
  “Recall that cells and the medium-to-large proteins cannot pass between the podocyte processes or through the fenestrations of the capillary endothelial cells.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 25.5 Physiology of Urine Formation, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/25-5-physiology-of-urine-formation)
