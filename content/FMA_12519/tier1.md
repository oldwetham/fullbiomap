The atlas holds up the skull on top of the vertebral column, or spine. The atlas is the first of the seven cervical vertebrae, the bones of the spine in the neck. The atlas is a ring of bone, made of a front arch and a back arch. When you shake your head “no,” the atlas turns on top of the axis, the second cervical vertebra.
