Each median antebrachial vein carries blood that drains from the hand and fingers. The median antebrachial vein runs alongside the ulnar vein, which follows the bones of the forearm. In the forearm, or antebrachium, the median antebrachial vein joins the basilic vein. The basilic vein is a surface vein that runs up into the upper arm.
