Each optic nerve carries visual information from the retina, the eye's light-sensing layer, to the brain. It is the second cranial nerve, one of twelve nerves connected to the brain. It begins at the very back of the eye, in a blind spot with no light-sensing cells. The two optic nerves meet at the optic chiasm, where some fibers cross to the other side of the brain.
