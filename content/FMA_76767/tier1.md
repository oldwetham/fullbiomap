The anterior cardiac veins drain blood from the front of the right ventricle, the heart's lower right pumping chamber. Most heart veins drain into the coronary sinus, a large vein on the back of the heart. But the anterior cardiac veins bypass the coronary sinus and drain straight into the right atrium. The right atrium is an upper chamber of the heart that receives blood.
