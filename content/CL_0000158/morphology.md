# Club cell: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Surface:** microvilli  
  “A club cell has short microvilli but no cilia.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000158 club cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000158)
- **Structure and job:** It is a secretory cell in the epithelium lining the trachea and bronchial tree, and not a mucus-secreting one.  
  “A non-mucous, epithelial secretory cell that is part of the tracheobronchial tree.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000158 club cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000158)
- **Structure and job:** Because it can divide and differentiate into ciliated cells, a club cell can regenerate the epithelium lining the bronchioles.  
  “A club cell is able to multiply and differentiate into ciliated cells to regenerate the bronchiolar epithelium and it also protects the tracheobronchial epithelium.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000158 club cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000158)
- **Structure and job:** It also protects the epithelium lining the trachea and bronchial tree.  
  “A club cell is able to multiply and differentiate into ciliated cells to regenerate the bronchiolar epithelium and it also protects the tracheobronchial epithelium.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000158 club cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000158)
