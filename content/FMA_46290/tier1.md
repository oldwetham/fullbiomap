The suprahyoid muscles help you swallow and speak. They pull up on the hyoid bone, a small U-shaped bone in the upper neck. When you swallow, these muscles also raise the floor of the mouth and the larynx, or voice box. The suprahyoid muscles are the neck muscles that sit above the hyoid bone.
