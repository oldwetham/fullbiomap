# Fibroblast: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Structure and job:** Fibroblasts secrete all three main kinds of connective tissue fiber: collagen, elastic and reticular fibers.  
  “Three main types of fibers are secreted by fibroblasts: collagen fibers, elastic fibers, and reticular fibers.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 4.3 Connective Tissue Supports and Protects, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/4-3-connective-tissue-supports-and-protects)
- **Structure and job:** The polysaccharides and proteins that fibroblasts release mix with extracellular fluid into a viscous ground substance, which together with the embedded fibers forms the extracellular matrix.  
  “Polysaccharides and proteins secreted by fibroblasts combine with extra-cellular fluids to produce a viscous ground substance that, with embedded fibrous proteins, forms the extra-cellular matrix.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 4.3 Connective Tissue Supports and Protects, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/4-3-connective-tissue-supports-and-protects)
- **Structure and job:** Fibroblasts secrete the ground substance, made of proteins and the polysaccharide hyaluronic acid, in which the connective tissue fibers are embedded.  
  “All of these fiber types are embedded in ground substance. Secreted by fibroblasts, ground substance is made of polysaccharides, specifically hyaluronic acid, and proteins.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 4.3 Connective Tissue Supports and Protects, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/4-3-connective-tissue-supports-and-protects)
- **Structure and job:** Fibroblasts can move, divide by mitosis and make whichever type of connective tissue is needed.  
  “Fibroblasts are motile, able to carry out mitosis, and can synthesize whichever connective tissue is needed.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 33.2 Animal Primary Tissues, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/33-2-animal-primary-tissues)
- **Structure and job:** In a healing wound, fibroblasts mobilize and divide rapidly, depositing collagen that forms granulation tissue to repair the damaged tissue.  
  “fibroblasts mobilize and divide rapidly to repair the damaged tissue by collagen deposition, forming granulation tissue.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 5.4 Diseases, Disorders, and Injuries of the Integumentary System, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/5-4-diseases-disorders-and-injuries-of-the-integumentary-system)
- **Structure and job:** Fibroblasts lay down the collagen of scar tissue, whose basket-weave of fibers repairs skin without restoring its normal cellular structure.  
  “Fibroblasts generate scar tissue in the form of collagen, and the bulk of repair is due to the basket-weave pattern generated by collagen fibers and does not result in regeneration of the typical cellular structure of skin.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 5.4 Diseases, Disorders, and Injuries of the Integumentary System, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/5-4-diseases-disorders-and-injuries-of-the-integumentary-system)
- **Structure and job:** When a bone breaks, fibroblasts produce collagen fibers that connect the broken ends.  
  “Fibroblasts produce collagen fibers that connect the broken bone ends, and osteoblasts start to form spongy bone.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 38.2 Bone, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/38-2-bone)
- **Special structure:** collagen fibers (extracellular, secreted by the cell)  
  “Three main types of fibers are secreted by fibroblasts: collagen fibers, elastic fibers, and reticular fibers.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 4.3 Connective Tissue Supports and Protects, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/4-3-connective-tissue-supports-and-protects)
