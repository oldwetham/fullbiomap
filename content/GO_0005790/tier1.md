The smooth endoplasmic reticulum (smooth ER) makes lipids, the group of molecules that includes fats and steroids. The smooth ER also stores calcium and detoxifies medicines and poisons. The smooth ER is part of the endoplasmic reticulum, a network of linked membrane tubes in the cell. The smooth ER has few or no ribosomes, the cell parts that build proteins.
