Each pectoralis major crosses the shoulder joint to move the humerus, the upper arm bone. The pectoralis major adducts the arm, drawing it toward or across the middle of the body. This thick, fan-shaped muscle covers much of the upper front of the chest. The pectoralis major is a convergent muscle: its fiber bundles spread wide, then meet in a tendon on the humerus.
