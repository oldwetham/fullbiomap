The inner segment handles the everyday work of a photoreceptor, an eye cell that responds to light. The inner segment holds the nucleus and other organelles, small structures that carry out cell functions. But the photoreceptor's other part, the outer segment, is where light is sensed. The inner segment also builds opsins, light-sensitive proteins, and passes them to the outer segment.
