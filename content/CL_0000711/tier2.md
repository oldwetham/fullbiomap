A cumulus granulosa cell, also called a cumulus cell, surrounds and nourishes the oocyte, or egg. The cumulus cell is a specialized granulosa cell, a type of supporting cell in the ovary. In the ovary, each oocyte develops surrounded by supporting cells; an oocyte together with these cells is called a follicle. Cumulus cells surround the fully grown oocyte to form the cumulus-oocyte complex, or COC. Names such as cumulus granulosa cell set this cell apart from a second group of granulosa cells that does a different job. That second group sits at the wall of the Graafian follicle, a mature follicle that bulges like a blister on the surface of the ovary.
