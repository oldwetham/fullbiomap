Each external oblique helps you twist at the waist and bend to the side. This muscle also squeezes the abdomen and pulls the chest downward. The external oblique is the outermost of three flat muscles in the front and side wall of the abdomen. This outermost muscle runs downward and toward the midline, like fingers sliding into pants pockets.
