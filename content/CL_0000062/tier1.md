Osteoblasts are the bone cells that build new bone. They are found in growing parts of bone, such as the periosteum, a bone's fibrous outer covering. Osteoblasts release bone matrix, the nonliving material between cells, and add calcium to harden the matrix. Once trapped in the hardened matrix, an osteoblast becomes an osteocyte, a mature bone cell.
