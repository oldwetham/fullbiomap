Each subclavian vein collects blood from the axillary vein, the main vein draining the arm. Each subclavian vein lies deep in the chest, starting where the axillary vein enters from the armpit. Each subclavian vein then joins veins from the head and neck to form a brachiocephalic vein. The subclavian veins are also where lymph, extra fluid drained from body tissues, flows back into the blood.
