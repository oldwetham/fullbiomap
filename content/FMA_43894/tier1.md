Each anterior tibial artery feeds the muscles and skin on the front of the lower leg. This artery branches off the popliteal artery, which runs down behind the knee. The anterior tibial artery lies between the tibia and fibula, the bones of the lower leg. At the ankle, this artery becomes the dorsalis pedis artery, which supplies the top of the foot.
