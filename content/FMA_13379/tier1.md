The rhomboid major holds the scapula, or shoulder blade, steady as the shoulder moves. This muscle also pulls the shoulder blade back toward the spine, a movement called retraction. Each rhomboid major links the shoulder blade to the thoracic vertebrae, the spine bones of the upper back. The word rhomboid means the muscle is shaped like a rhombus, with equal and parallel sides.
