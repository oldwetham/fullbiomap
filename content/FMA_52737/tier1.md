Each temporal bone helps form the brain case, the part of the skull that surrounds and protects the brain. Each temporal bone forms the lower part of one side of the skull. Inside each temporal bone is the inner ear, which contains the sense organs for hearing and balance. The mandible, or lower jaw, joins each temporal bone at a joint that lets the mouth open and close.
