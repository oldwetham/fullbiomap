The subscapularis turns the arm inward, a movement called medial rotation. The subscapularis is one of four rotator cuff muscles, which wrap around and support the shoulder joint. These muscles hold the top of the humerus, the upper arm bone, in the shoulder socket. The subscapularis starts on the front surface of the scapula, or shoulder blade.
