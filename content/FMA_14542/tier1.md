With its lymphoid tissue, clusters of immune cells, the appendix may help the immune system. Still, most see the appendix as vestigial: a leftover from a distant ancestor, with no clear use. However, one report suggests a possible use: storing gut bacteria to restock the gut after diarrheal illness. The appendix is a coiled tube attached to the cecum, the pouch where the large intestine begins.
