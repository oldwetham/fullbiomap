The metencephalon includes the cerebellum, which controls balance and helps coordinate movement. The metencephalon also includes the pons, the cerebellum's main link to the rest of the brain. The metencephalon is part of the hindbrain, the back region of the brain. In the embryo, the metencephalon grows into the pons and the cerebellum.
