Each main bronchus is a tube that carries air into and out of a lung. The trachea, or windpipe, splits into the right and left main bronchi. Rings of cartilage, a firm but flexible tissue, keep each main bronchus from collapsing. Each main bronchus is lined with cells that make mucus, a sticky fluid that traps tiny particles.
