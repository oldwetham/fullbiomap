Each opponens pollicis moves the thumb across the palm toward the other fingers. This movement, called opposition, brings the tip of the thumb to the tip of a finger. Thumb movements like this play a key part in most precise movements of the hand. The opponens pollicis is a thenar muscle, part of the fleshy mound at the base of the thumb.
