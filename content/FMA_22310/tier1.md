Each iliacus flexes the thigh, lifting the thigh forward and up at the hip. With the psoas major muscle, the iliacus forms a group called the iliopsoas. The iliacus lies in the iliac fossa, a shallow dip on the inner surface of the hip bone. The iliacus also helps form the back wall of the abdomen.
