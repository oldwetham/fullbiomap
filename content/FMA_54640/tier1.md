The tongue helps you taste, chew, swallow and speak. The tongue is a muscular organ in the floor of the mouth. Tongue muscles gather food into a bolus, a rounded mass of chewed food, for swallowing. The tongue is covered with papillae, small bumps that provide grip and hold taste buds.
