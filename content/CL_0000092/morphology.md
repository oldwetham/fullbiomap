# Osteoclast: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Nuclei:** many (up to 50)  
  “Osteoclasts are large bone cells with up to 50 nuclei.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 38.2 Bone, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/38-2-bone)
- **Nuclei:** many  
  “They are found on bone surfaces, are multinucleated, and originate from monocytes and macrophages, two types of white blood cells, not from osteogenic cells.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.3 Bone Structure, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-3-bone-structure)
- **Structure and job:** Large and carrying up to 50 nuclei, osteoclasts take bone apart by releasing lysosomal enzymes and acids that dissolve the bony matrix.  
  “Osteoclasts are large bone cells with up to 50 nuclei. They remove bone structure by releasing lysosomal enzymes and acids that dissolve the bony matrix.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 38.2 Bone, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/38-2-bone)
- **Structure and job:** The osteoclast is a multinucleated cell that sits on bone surfaces and is the cell responsible for breaking bone down (resorption).  
  “The cell responsible for bone resorption, or breakdown, is the osteoclast. They are found on bone surfaces, are multinucleated, and originate from monocytes and macrophages, two types of white blood cells, not from osteogenic cells.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.3 Bone Structure, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-3-bone-structure)
- **Structure and job:** When parathyroid hormone stimulates them, osteoclasts secrete enzymes that degrade bone, which releases its calcium into the interstitial fluid.  
  “PTH secretion causes the release of calcium from the bones by stimulating osteoclasts, which secrete enzymes that degrade bone and release calcium into the interstitial fluid.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 17.5 The Parathyroid Glands, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/17-5-the-parathyroid-glands)
- **Structure and job:** A specialized phagocytic cell, usually differentiated from monocytes, that takes part in absorbing and removing the mineralized matrix of bone tissue.  
  “A specialized phagocytic cell associated with the absorption and removal of the mineralized matrix of bone tissue, which typically differentiates from monocytes.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000092 osteoclast, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000092)
