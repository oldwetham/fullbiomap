The rectum stores feces, the semisolid waste of digestion, until they leave the body. Waste from the colon enters the rectum, which lies in the pelvis. As the rectum fills and stretches, nerve signals create the urge to pass feces. Feces are pushed out of the body when the muscles of the rectum contract.
