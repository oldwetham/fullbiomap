The pancreatic A cell, or alpha cell, releases glucagon, a hormone that raises blood sugar levels. This cell sits at the edge of the pancreatic islets, clusters of cells that release hormones. Alpha cells release glucagon when blood sugar drops, such as between meals or during exercise. Glucagon makes the liver send more sugar into the blood.
