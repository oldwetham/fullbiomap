The sphenoid bone joins with most of the other bones of the skull. Shaped like a butterfly, the sphenoid bone lies at the base of the skull. A groove in the sphenoid bone holds the pituitary gland, a small organ that makes hormones. The sphenoid bone also forms the back of each orbit, or eye socket.
