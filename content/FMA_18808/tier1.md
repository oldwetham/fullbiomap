Each internal iliac artery brings blood to the pelvis and the inner thigh. Branches of this artery reach the bladder, the pelvic walls and the external genitals. In females, this artery also supplies the uterus and vagina. Each internal iliac artery is a short, thick branch of a common iliac artery, which feeds the pelvis and legs.
