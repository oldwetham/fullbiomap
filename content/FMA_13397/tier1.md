The serratus anterior moves the arm from the side of the body to the front. This muscle pulls the shoulder blade forward, a movement called protraction. The muscle also lifts the ribs and helps with inhalation, which draws air into the lungs. This large, flat muscle runs from the ribs to the scapula, or shoulder blade.
