Specific granules are stores of chemicals inside a cell, but most are released from the cell. Specific granules hold lysozyme, which breaks down the cell walls of bacteria. These granules are found mainly in mature neutrophils, white blood cells that are especially active against bacteria. Inside each specific granule is a tube-like structure made of membrane.
