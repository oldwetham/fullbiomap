Endothelial cells form the smooth inner lining of blood vessels. In capillaries, the smallest blood vessels, gases pass right through these cells. These cells also release nitric oxide, which causes vasodilation, the widening of blood vessels. But these cells also release endothelins, chemicals that cause vasoconstriction, the narrowing of blood vessels.
