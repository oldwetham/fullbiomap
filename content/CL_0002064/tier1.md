Pancreatic acinar cells release digestive enzymes, proteins that speed up chemical reactions in the body. These cells form small grape-like clusters, called acini, in the pancreas, an organ behind the stomach. The enzymes help break down sugars, proteins and fats. Enzymes that digest protein are released in an inactive form, so they do not digest the pancreas.
