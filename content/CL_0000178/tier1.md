A Leydig cell makes testosterone, a hormone that helps the body make sperm. Sperm form inside tubes in the testis called seminiferous tubules, while Leydig cells sit between these tubes. In a male embryo, testosterone from Leydig cells helps the male sex organs develop. From adolescence on, Leydig cells make high levels of testosterone.
