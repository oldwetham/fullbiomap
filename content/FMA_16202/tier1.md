The sacrum firmly links each hip bone to the spine. The sacrum forms the back of the pelvis, a ring of bone that includes the hip bones. Low in the spine, the sacrum is a triangular bone made of five bones fused into one. The sacrum's wide top bears weight, but its narrow bottom tip does not.
