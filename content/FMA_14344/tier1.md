Each testicular vein carries blood away from a testis, one of the male organs that make sperm. This vein is part of the spermatic cord, a bundle of nerves and blood vessels that supplies the testes. The right testicular vein empties straight into the inferior vena cava, a large vein that returns blood to the heart. But the left testicular vein empties into the left renal vein, a vein that carries blood out of the kidney.
