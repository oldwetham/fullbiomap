The levator palpebrae superioris lifts the upper eyelid, which opens the eye. This muscle runs from the roof of the orbit, the bony eye socket, to the skin of the upper eyelid. The oculomotor nerve controls this muscle and four of the muscles that move the eye. The upper eyelid usually lifts at the same time as the eye looks up.
