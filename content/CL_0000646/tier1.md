A basal cell is a stem cell that divides to make other kinds of epithelial cells, which cover and line the body. In the skin, basal cells constantly divide to make keratinocytes, the most common cells in the skin's outer layer. As new cells form, older cells get pushed up toward the skin's surface. Basal cells in the hair bulb, at the base of each hair's root, make keratinocytes to start hair growth.
