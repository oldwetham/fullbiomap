Platelets help seal a torn blood vessel and stop further blood loss. At the damaged spot, many platelets stick together to form a plug. A platelet is not a whole cell but a piece of a bone marrow cell called a megakaryocyte. Many platelets are stored in the spleen, a blood-filtering organ, while others travel in the bloodstream.
