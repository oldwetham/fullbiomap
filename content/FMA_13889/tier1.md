The pituitary gland makes, stores and releases hormones, substances that travel in the blood to act on other body parts. Some of these hormones turn other hormone-making glands on or off. Sometimes called the “master gland,” the pituitary sits at the base of the brain in a groove of a skull bone. The gland hangs from the hypothalamus, a part of the brain that tells the gland when to release hormones.
