The corpus callosum is the main pathway for messages between the two halves of the cerebrum, the brain's largest part. This pathway lies in the gap that separates the left and right halves. The corpus callosum is a wide, flat bundle of 200 to 250 million nerve fibers. This bundle lets the two halves work together on movement, senses and thinking.
