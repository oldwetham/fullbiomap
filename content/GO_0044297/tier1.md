The cell body holds the nucleus, which contains the cell's DNA. Extensions called processes reach out from the cell body, but they are not part of it. Astrocytes, star-shaped cells that support nerve cells, have many processes reaching out from the main cell body. By contrast, an oligodendrocyte, a cell that insulates nerve fibers, has just a few processes.
