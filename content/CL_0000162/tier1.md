A parietal cell makes hydrochloric acid, the digestive acid in the stomach. This acid kills much of the bacteria in food and helps get proteins ready for digestion. A parietal cell also makes intrinsic factor, which the small intestine needs to absorb vitamin B12. Parietal cells sit in the gastric glands, which make the stomach's digestive juice.
