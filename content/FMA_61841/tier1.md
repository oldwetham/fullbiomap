Each amygdala helps you remember and feel emotions. Each amygdala lies deep inside a temporal lobe, a part of the brain by your ears. Together, the two amygdalae are key to sensing fear and recognizing fearful faces. When you are scared, the amygdala sends signals that start the fight-or-flight response, which helps you flee or face a threat.
