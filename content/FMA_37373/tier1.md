Each abductor pollicis brevis moves the thumb away from the body. This movement, called abduction, swings the thumb forward to point straight out from the palm. This muscle belongs to the thenar muscles, which form the rounded bulge at the base of the thumb. Like the other thenar muscles, the abductor pollicis brevis starts and ends within the hand.
