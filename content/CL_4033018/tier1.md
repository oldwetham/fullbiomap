Megakaryocytes break apart to form platelets, small cell pieces that plug wounds to prevent blood loss. Lung megakaryocytes are megakaryocytes that live in the lung's connective tissue, a tissue that holds organs in place. Megakaryocytes are large cells with an enlarged nucleus.
