The external iliac vein is a large vein that carries blood away from the leg. This vein lies in the lower torso, beside the external iliac artery, the leg's main blood supply. The external iliac vein begins where the femoral vein, which drains the upper leg, crosses the body wall. The external iliac vein then merges with the internal iliac vein from the pelvis, forming the common iliac vein.
