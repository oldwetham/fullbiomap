Each renal vein carries blood away from a kidney, an organ that filters the blood and removes wastes. Each renal vein leaves its kidney at the hilum, the curved-in part of the kidney's bean shape. The renal vein then empties the cleaned blood into the inferior vena cava, a large vein leading to the heart. The left renal vein is longer than the right, because the inferior vena cava lies mostly to the right of the spine.
