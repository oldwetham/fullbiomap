Melanosomes are where melanin, the pigment that colors skin and hair, is made and stored. Melanosomes form inside melanocytes, skin cells that make melanin. Melanosomes then carry melanin into keratinocytes, the main cells of the skin's outer layer. This transfer of melanin helps protect cells from UV rays.
