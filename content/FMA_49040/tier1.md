The inferior oblique is one of six extraocular muscles, the muscles that move the eye. This muscle mainly twists the eyeball but can also move the eye up and away from the nose. In this twist, the top of the eyeball tips toward the side, like a clock hand. This muscle runs from the floor of the orbit, the bony eye socket, to the eye's lower, outer side.
