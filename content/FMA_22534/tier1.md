The extensor digitorum longus extends, or straightens, the second through fifth toes. This muscle also helps raise the front of the foot, a movement called dorsiflexion. The extensor digitorum longus sits at the front of the leg, between the knee and the ankle. This muscle runs from the tibia and fibula, the leg bones, to the second through fifth toes.
