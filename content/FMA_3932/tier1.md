The brachiocephalic artery carries blood to the head, neck, arm and chest wall. Found only on the right side, it is the first branch off the arch of the aorta, the body's largest artery. One branch of the brachiocephalic artery, the right common carotid artery, supplies the head and neck. The other branch, the right subclavian artery, supplies the right arm and chest.
