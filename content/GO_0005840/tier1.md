Ribosomes are the parts of a cell that build proteins. Nearly every cell has them, and cells that make lots of protein have especially many. Ribosomes get their instructions from messenger RNA, a molecule that carries the code for making a protein. Each ribosome has a small part and a large part, which come together on the messenger RNA.
