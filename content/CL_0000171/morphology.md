# Pancreatic A cell: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Structure and job:** A minority cell in each pancreatic islet (about a fifth of its cells), it makes glucagon and releases it when blood glucose is low.  
  “The alpha cell produces the hormone glucagon and makes up approximately 20 percent of each islet. Glucagon plays an important role in blood glucose regulation; low blood glucose levels stimulate its release.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 17.9 The Endocrine Pancreas, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/17-9-the-endocrine-pancreas)
- **Structure and job:** It sits at the outer edge (periphery) of the islets of Langerhans, where it secretes glucagon.  
  “A type A enteroendocrine cell found in the periphery of the islets of Langerhans that secretes glucagon.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000171 pancreatic A cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000171)
- **Structure and job:** When blood glucose falls, the glucagon it releases raises blood glucose by speeding up glycogen breakdown and glucose release in the liver.  
  “As blood glucose levels decline, alpha cells release glucagon to raise the blood glucose levels by increasing rates of glycogen breakdown and glucose release by the liver.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 37.5 Endocrine Glands, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/37-5-endocrine-glands)
- **Structure and job:** It releases glucagon between meals or during exercise, when blood glucose drops below normal.  
  “When blood glucose levels decline below normal levels, for example between meals or when glucose is utilized rapidly during exercise, the hormone glucagon is released from the alpha cells of the pancreas.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 37.3 Regulation of Body Processes, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/37-3-regulation-of-body-processes)
- **Structure and job:** Its product, glucagon, is a protein hormone that increases blood glucose levels.  
  “Glucagon (alpha cells) | Protein | Increases blood glucose levels” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 17.9 The Endocrine Pancreas, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/17-9-the-endocrine-pancreas)
