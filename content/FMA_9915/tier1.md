The cervical vertebrae, the seven bones of the spine in the neck, give the neck a wide range of motion. Cervical vertebrae carry the least body weight, so they have small bodies, the weight-bearing front parts. An artery that supplies the brain runs up the neck through openings in the cervical vertebrae.
