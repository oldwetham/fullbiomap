Each omohyoid pulls down the hyoid bone, a small U-shaped bone in the upper neck. The omohyoid is an infrahyoid muscle, one of the front neck muscles below the hyoid bone. Each omohyoid runs from the top edge of the scapula, or shoulder blade, to the hyoid bone. This muscle has two fleshy parts, called bellies: an upper belly and a lower belly.
