The cerebral aqueduct is a narrow channel linking two ventricles, fluid-filled spaces inside the brain. Cerebrospinal fluid, a clear cushioning liquid, flows through the aqueduct from the third ventricle to the fourth. The aqueduct runs through the center of the midbrain, the middle region of the brain. Two parts of the midbrain form the aqueduct's roof and floor.
