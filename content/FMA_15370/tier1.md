Lumbar veins drain blood from the lumbar, or lower back, parts of the abdominal wall and spinal cord. There are usually four lumbar veins on each side. Blood from the upper lumbar veins reaches the superior vena cava, which drains the upper body. The other lumbar veins, however, empty directly into the inferior vena cava, which drains the lower body.
