Each globus pallidus helps control movement and lies deep inside the brain. It belongs to the basal nuclei, linked groups of brain cells involved in movement and motivation. It sits next to the putamen, another basal nucleus, and they fit together like curved lenses. It has two parts, an outer segment and an inner segment.
