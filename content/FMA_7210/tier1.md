Each testis makes sperm and male hormones such as testosterone. Each testis sits in the scrotum, a pouch of skin and muscle outside the body. This location matters because sperm form best when the testes are a little cooler than the body's core. Inside each testis, sperm are made in tightly coiled tubes called seminiferous tubules.
