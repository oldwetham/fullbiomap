The skin protects the body, senses changes in the environment, helps control body temperature and makes vitamin D. The skin surrounds the entire body and forms the body's outer layer. The skin's outermost layer, the epidermis, is fairly thin and has no blood supply. In contrast, the dermis below the epidermis is much thicker and holds blood vessels and sweat glands.
