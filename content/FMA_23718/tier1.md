Each pisiform helps anchor a strong ligament that holds the wrist bones in a U-shaped group. A muscle that moves the little finger away from the body also attaches to the pisiform. The pisiform is one of eight carpal bones, the small bones of the wrist. The pisiform sticks out forward, forming a bony bump you can feel at the base of your hand.
