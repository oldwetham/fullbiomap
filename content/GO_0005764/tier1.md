Lysosomes act as the cell's garbage disposal, breaking down worn-out cell parts. Lysosomes do this with digestive enzymes, which are proteins that speed up chemical reactions. White blood cells that engulf bacteria are packed with lysosomes, which digest the bacteria. New lysosomes form from sacs of enzymes sent out by the Golgi apparatus, which packages proteins.
