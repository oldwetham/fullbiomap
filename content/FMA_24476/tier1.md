The tibia, or shin bone, is the main weight-bearing bone of the lower leg. Along the inner side of the leg, you can feel the tibia just under the skin. The top of the tibia meets the femur, or thigh bone, to form the knee joint. At the ankle, the bottom of the tibia meets the talus, a bone of the foot.
