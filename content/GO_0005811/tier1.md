Lipid droplets store lipids, a group that includes fats and oils, inside cells. White fat cells hold their lipids in one large drop, while brown fat cells hold many droplets. Heart muscle cells also store droplets of fatty acids, parts of fat molecules, as an extra supply of nutrients. Each droplet's lipids are wrapped in a single layer of phospholipids, the main part of membranes.
