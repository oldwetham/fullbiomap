Cardiac muscle cells, also called cardiomyocytes, do the heart's pumping and make up the heart muscle. They can contract in their own built-in rhythm, with no outside signal. Neighboring cells are joined by intercalated discs, junctions that help pass electrical signals from one cell to the next. Because the cells are linked physically and electrically, the whole heart contracts as one unit.
