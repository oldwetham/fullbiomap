The cytoplasm is where many of a cell's chemical reactions take place, including making proteins. The cytoplasm fills the space between the cell membrane and the nucleus, the cell's control center. Most of the cytoplasm is cytosol, a clear, watery fluid. Organelles, little structures with specific jobs, are suspended in the cytoplasm.
