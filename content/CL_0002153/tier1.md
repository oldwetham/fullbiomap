Corneocytes help keep the body from drying out, forming a layer of dead cells on the skin's surface. This dead layer is too dry for bacteria to grow in. Each corneocyte is flat, has no nucleus, and is filled with keratin, a protein that makes skin hard and water-resistant. Corneocytes regularly flake away and are replaced by cells from deeper layers.
