Each optic tract passes visual information on toward the brain's main vision area. Each optic tract starts at the optic chiasm, where some fibers from the eyes cross to the other side of the brain. Most connections of the optic tract go to the thalamus, a brain area that relays sensory information. The optic tract also reaches the superior colliculus, a brain area that manages eye movements.
