Hepatocytes, the main cells of the liver, process, store and release nutrients into the blood. Packed tightly around the liver's open blood spaces, hepatocytes have easy access to the blood. Hepatocytes make bile, a digestive juice that helps the body digest fats. Hepatocytes also make many toxins harmless.
