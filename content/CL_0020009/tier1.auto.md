A GABAergic neuron located in the external globus pallidus (GPe) that constitutes approximately two-thirds of all GPe neurons (Mallet et al., 2012, Dodson et al., 2015). Derived from the medial ganglionic eminence, this cell selectively expresses Nkx2-1, with roughly two-thirds co-expressing parvalbumin (PV) and one-third lacking it in mice (Mallet et al., 2012; Dodson et al., 2015).
