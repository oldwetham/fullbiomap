A projection neuron that has its soma located in the internal segment of the globus pallidus (GPi), in the peripheral 'shell' region. In mice and primates, it expresses somatostatin and shows dual expression of GABAergic and glutamatergic markers, enabling co-release of GABA and glutamate (Wallace et al., 2017). It projects primarily to the lateral habenula (Parent & De Bellefeuille, 1982; Wallace et al., 2017), forming the limbic/aversive output stream of the GPi.
