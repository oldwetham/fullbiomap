Each longissimus thoracis extends the spine, which means bending the spine backward or straightening it up. Each longissimus thoracis attaches to the ribs and to vertebrae, the bones of the spine. This muscle is part of the erector spinae, the large muscle mass of the back. The word longissimus means “longest.”
