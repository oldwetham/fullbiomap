Myofibrils are the parts of skeletal muscle and heart muscle that contract. Each myofibril is a long rod that runs lengthwise inside a muscle cell. A myofibril is a row of many sarcomeres, the smallest contracting parts of a muscle. When the sarcomeres contract, the myofibril and the whole muscle cell shorten.
