A fibroblast makes the fibers in nearly all connective tissues, the tissues that hold organs in place. Fibroblasts are the most common cells in connective tissue. Fibroblasts make collagen fibers, which are flexible and give the tissue strength. Fibroblasts can also move, divide, and make whatever connective tissue is needed.
