Osteoclasts are the cells that break down bone, a process called resorption. They dissolve old or unneeded bone, and this can release calcium. Osteoclasts are found on bone surfaces and come from two types of white blood cells. Each osteoclast is a large cell with up to 50 nuclei, the cell's control centers.
