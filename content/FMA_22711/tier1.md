Each longissimus cervicis extends, or straightens, the spine in the neck. This muscle runs from the spine in the upper back to the middle and upper neck. The longissimus cervicis is part of the erector spinae, the back muscles that keep the body upright. This muscle belongs to the longissimus group, the middle muscles of the erector spinae.
