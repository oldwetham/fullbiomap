The adductor brevis adducts the thigh, pulling the thigh toward the body's midline. The adductor brevis can also rotate the thigh inward or outward, depending on the foot's position. The adductor brevis lies on the inner thigh, along with other adductor muscles. In the name adductor brevis, brevis means “short.”
