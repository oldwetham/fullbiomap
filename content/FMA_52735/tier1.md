The occipital bone forms the back of the brain case, which holds and protects the brain. The occipital bone also forms part of the base of the skull. There, a large hole called the foramen magnum lets the spinal cord leave the skull. Oval knobs beside this hole hold the skull on top of the vertebral column, or backbone.
