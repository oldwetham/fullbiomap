The posterior cerebral artery supplies blood to the back of the cerebrum and to the brain stem. The cerebrum is the largest part of the brain. The brain stem is the part of the brain that connects with the spinal cord and controls breathing. The posterior cerebral artery forms part of the circle of Willis, a group of blood vessels around the base of the brain.
