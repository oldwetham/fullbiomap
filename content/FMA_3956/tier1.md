Each vertebral artery carries blood to the brain and spinal cord. This artery runs up through openings in the cervical vertebrae, the bones of the spine in the neck. The vertebral artery enters the skull through the foramen magnum, a large opening that the spinal cord passes through. Both vertebral arteries then merge into the basilar artery, which feeds the brain stem, the part of the brain joined to the spinal cord.
