Each occipital lobe works mainly on vision: seeing, recognizing and identifying the world we see. It sits at the back of the brain. It is a region of the cerebral cortex, the folded outer layer of the brain. Signals from the eyes reach its primary visual cortex, where basic shapes start to be recognized.
