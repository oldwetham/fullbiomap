Each flexor digitorum superficialis flexes, or bends, the fingers and the hand at the wrist. The muscle bends the fingers at the knuckles, allowing quick finger movements, as in typing or playing an instrument. This muscle belongs to the group of muscles near the surface at the front of the forearm.
