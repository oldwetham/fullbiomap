Through its branches, the descending aorta sends blood to the internal organs. The descending aorta is the part of the aorta, the body's largest artery, that runs down through the chest and abdomen. The descending aorta passes through the diaphragm, the muscle between the chest and the abdomen. The part above the diaphragm is called the thoracic aorta, and the part below is the abdominal aorta.
