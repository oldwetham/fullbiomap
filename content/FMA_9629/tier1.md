The supraspinatus helps abduct the arm, raising the arm out to the side, away from the body. The supraspinatus runs from the scapula, or shoulder blade, to the humerus, or arm bone. The muscle is named for its place above a ridge called the spine of the scapula. The supraspinatus is one of four rotator cuff muscles, whose tendons circle and support the shoulder.
