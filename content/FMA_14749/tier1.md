The superior mesenteric artery is the main blood supply of the small intestine, where most digestion and absorption take place. Branches of this artery also feed most of the large intestine, the last part of the digestive tract. The pancreas, an organ behind the stomach, gets blood from this artery too. In the abdomen, this artery branches off the aorta, the body's largest artery.
