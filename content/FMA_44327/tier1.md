The popliteal vein collects blood from the calf and the knee joint. Like every vein, the popliteal vein carries blood toward the heart. The popliteal vein forms where the anterior and posterior tibial veins and the fibular vein join. The popliteal vein passes behind the knee and becomes the femoral vein, which drains the upper leg.
