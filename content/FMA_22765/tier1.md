Each spinalis thoracis supports and extends the spine, bending the spine back or bringing it upright. Each spinalis thoracis is a spinalis muscle of the thoracic region, the upper back. The spinalis muscles are part of the erector spinae, the large muscle mass of the back. Each spinalis thoracis runs between spinous processes, the bumps you can feel down the middle of the back.
