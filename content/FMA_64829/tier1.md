The scalene muscles work together to tilt the head forward or sideways and to turn it. During a forceful breath in, the scalene muscles help lift the chest wall to expand the lungs. The scalene muscles link the lower cervical vertebrae, the spine's neck bones, to the first two ribs. From front to back, the scalene muscles are the anterior, middle and posterior scalenes.
