The cell body is the main part of a neuron, or nerve cell, and carries out the cell's general functions. The cell body, also called the soma, holds the nucleus and most of the major organelles, the cell's small working parts. Extensions called dendrites carry signals to the cell body. Another extension, called an axon, carries signals away from the cell body.
