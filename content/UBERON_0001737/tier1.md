The larynx makes your voice and keeps food and drink out of the trachea, or windpipe. Also called the voice box, the larynx links the pharynx, or throat, to the trachea. The larynx holds the vocal cords, two folded membranes that vibrate as air passes to make sound. When you swallow, a leaf-shaped part of the larynx called the epiglottis swings to close the trachea.
