Each flexor digitorum longus curls the four small toes into the foot. The flexor digitorum longus lies deep in the back of the lower leg. The flexor digitorum longus starts on the tibia, the shin bone. The muscle ends in four tendons, strong bands linking muscle to bone, one per small toe.
