The limbic lobe is a group of brain structures involved in memory and emotion. These structures sit around the edges of the cerebrum, the largest part of the brain. The limbic lobe has strong links to the hypothalamus, a brain structure that controls hormone release. When you are scared, the amygdala, a part of the limbic lobe, signals the hypothalamus.
