# Chondrocyte: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Special structure:** lacuna (the space in the cartilage matrix that the cell occupies)  
  “Embedded within the cartilage matrix are chondrocytes, or cartilage cells, and the space they occupy are called lacunae (singular = lacuna).” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 4.3 Connective Tissue Supports and Protects, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/4-3-connective-tissue-supports-and-protects)
- **Special structure:** surrounding cartilage matrix (specialized, avascular, GAG-rich) in which the cell is embedded  
  “secretes a specialized, avascular, GAG-rich matrix, is embedded in cartilage tissue matrix” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000138 chondrocyte, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000138)
- **Structure and job:** Chondrocytes are the cells that produce cartilage's matrix and its fibers.  
  “The cells, called chondrocytes, make the matrix and fibers of the tissue.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 33.2 Animal Primary Tissues, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/33-2-animal-primary-tissues)
- **Structure and job:** A chondroblast becomes a chondrocyte once the matrix that chondroblasts produce (hyaluronic acid, chondroitin sulfate, collagen fibers and water) surrounds and isolates it.  
  “This framework is a flexible, semi-solid matrix produced by chondroblasts and consists of hyaluronic acid, chondroitin sulfate, collagen fibers, and water. As the matrix surrounds and isolates chondroblasts, they are called chondrocytes.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.4 Bone Formation and Development, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-4-bone-formation-and-development)
- **Structure and job:** Cartilage has no blood vessels, so every nutrient must diffuse through the matrix to reach the chondrocytes, which is one reason cartilage heals very slowly.  
  “Cartilaginous tissue is avascular, thus all nutrients need to diffuse through the matrix to reach the chondrocytes. This is a factor contributing to the very slow healing of cartilaginous tissues.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 4.3 Connective Tissue Supports and Protects, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/4-3-connective-tissue-supports-and-protects)
- **Structure and job:** The chondrocyte secretes a specialized, avascular, GAG-rich matrix and still keeps the ability to divide while embedded in cartilage matrix.  
  “A skeletogenic cell that secretes a specialized, avascular, GAG-rich matrix, is embedded in cartilage tissue matrix, retains the ability to divide, and develops from a chondroblast cell.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000138 chondrocyte, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000138)
- **Structure and job:** In the growth plate's proliferative zone, stacked chondrocytes divide by mitosis to replace the ones that die at the diaphyseal end of the plate.  
  “The proliferative zone is the next layer toward the diaphysis and contains stacks of slightly larger chondrocytes. It makes new chondrocytes (via mitosis) to replace those that die at the diaphyseal end of the plate.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.4 Bone Formation and Development, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-4-bone-formation-and-development)
- **Structure and job:** Small chondrocytes in the growth plate's reserve zone take no part in bone growth; instead they anchor the plate to the bone of the epiphysis.  
  “The reserve zone is the region closest to the epiphyseal end of the plate and contains small chondrocytes within the matrix. These chondrocytes do not participate in bone growth but secure the epiphyseal plate to the osseous tissue of the epiphysis.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.4 Bone Formation and Development, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-4-bone-formation-and-development)
- **Structure and job:** Long bones lengthen because chondrocytes divide and secrete hyaline cartilage.  
  “Long bones lengthen as chondrocytes divide and secrete hyaline cartilage.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 38.2 Bone, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/38-2-bone)
- **Structure and job:** In a developing bone's cartilage model, the central chondrocytes enlarge; once their matrix calcifies, nutrients can no longer reach them, so they die and the cartilage around them breaks down.  
  “As more matrix is produced, the chondrocytes in the center of the cartilaginous model grow in size. As the matrix calcifies, nutrients can no longer reach the chondrocytes. This results in their death and the disintegration of the surrounding cartilage.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.4 Bone Formation and Development, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-4-bone-formation-and-development)
- **Structure and job:** After a fracture, chondrocytes from the endosteum secrete a fibrocartilage matrix between the broken ends, forming an internal callus within about 48 hours.  
  “Within about 48 hours after the fracture, chondrocytes from the endosteum create an internal callus (plural = calli) by secreting a fibrocartilaginous matrix between the two ends of the broken bone” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.5 Fractures: Bone Repair, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-5-fractures-bone-repair)
- **Structure and job:** Growth hormone from the pituitary makes the chondrocytes in the epiphyseal plates multiply, which lengthens long bones.  
  “The pituitary gland secretes growth hormone (GH), which, as its name implies, controls bone growth in several ways. It triggers chondrocyte proliferation in epiphyseal plates, resulting in the increasing length of long bones.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.6 Exercise, Nutrition, Hormones, and Bone Tissue, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-6-exercise-nutrition-hormones-and-bone-tissue)
