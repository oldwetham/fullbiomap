Each mylohyoid helps press the tongue to the top of the mouth. Each mylohyoid also lifts the hyoid bone, a small U-shaped bone in the upper neck. The mylohyoid muscles lie beneath the floor of the mouth. Each mylohyoid runs from the mandible, or lower jaw, to the hyoid bone.
