The ciliary ganglion helps control the size of the pupil, the opening that lets light into the eye. This ganglion, a cluster of nerve cell bodies, sits at the back of the eye socket. It belongs to the parasympathetic system, the part of the nervous system for rest and digestion. In bright light, its fibers make the iris, the colored part of the eye, narrow the pupil.
