Each sartorius is a muscle that lets us sit cross-legged. It flexes, or bends, both the knee and the hip. It also abducts the thigh, moving it out to the side, and turns it outward. This long, thin muscle runs from the pelvic bone to the top of the tibia, or shin bone.
