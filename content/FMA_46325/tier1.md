Each geniohyoid muscle pulls the hyoid bone, a U-shaped bone in the upper neck, forward when you swallow. The geniohyoid also lifts the hyoid bone and pulls down the mandible, the lower jaw bone. Each geniohyoid runs from the chin to the hyoid bone.
