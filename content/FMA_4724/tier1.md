Each internal jugular vein is a major vein that mainly drains blood from the brain. This vein passes through the jugular foramen, an opening in the skull's base where blood from the brain exits. In the neck, this vein runs beside the common carotid artery, which feeds the head and neck. Blood in this vein flows on toward the heart, to be pumped to the lungs for fresh oxygen.
