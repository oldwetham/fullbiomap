Each postcentral gyrus processes touch, pressure, tickle, pain, itch and vibration. The postcentral gyrus is a ridge in the parietal lobe, a region at the top of the brain. This ridge is where these sensations are first processed and consciously felt. Each postcentral gyrus receives its sensations from the opposite side of the body.
