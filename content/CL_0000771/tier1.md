An eosinophil is a white blood cell that helps protect the body against parasitic worms. Each eosinophil has many granules, which are stores of chemicals inside the cell. Some of these granules hold molecules that are toxic to the worms. Chemical signals draw eosinophils to worms in the gut, where the cells release their granule contents.
