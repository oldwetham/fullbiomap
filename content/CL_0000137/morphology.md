# Osteocyte: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Shape:** other: cell with long cytoplasmic processes  
  “Like osteoblasts, osteocytes lack mitotic activity. They can communicate with each other and receive nutrients via long cytoplasmic processes that extend through canaliculi (singular = canaliculus), channels within the bone matrix.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.3 Bone Structure, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-3-bone-structure)
- **Surface:** long processes  
  “Like osteoblasts, osteocytes lack mitotic activity. They can communicate with each other and receive nutrients via long cytoplasmic processes that extend through canaliculi (singular = canaliculus), channels within the bone matrix.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.3 Bone Structure, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-3-bone-structure)
- **Special structure:** lacuna (a small cavity in the bone matrix that holds the cell)  
  “They occupy a small cavity, called lacuna, in the matrix and are connected to adjacent osteocytes via protoplasmic projections called canaliculi.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000137 osteocyte, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000137)
- **Structure and job:** Its long cytoplasmic processes run through canaliculi, channels in the bone matrix, so osteocytes can communicate with each other and receive nutrients.  
  “Like osteoblasts, osteocytes lack mitotic activity. They can communicate with each other and receive nutrients via long cytoplasmic processes that extend through canaliculi (singular = canaliculus), channels within the bone matrix.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.3 Bone Structure, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-3-bone-structure)
- **Structure and job:** Housed in a lacuna inside bone tissue, each osteocyte secretes enzymes that maintain the mineral concentration of the bone matrix.  
  “Each osteocyte is located in a space called a lacuna and is surrounded by bone tissue. Osteocytes maintain the mineral concentration of the matrix via the secretion of enzymes.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.3 Bone Structure, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-3-bone-structure)
