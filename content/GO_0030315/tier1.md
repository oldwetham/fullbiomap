T tubules carry electrical signals from a muscle cell's surface to the inside of the cell. Each T tubule is an inward fold of the cell's outer membrane. Inside, the signal makes the nearby sarcoplasmic reticulum, the cell's calcium store, release calcium. This calcium starts the cell's contraction.
