The basilar artery is the main blood supply to the brain stem. The brain stem, the part of the brain that joins the spinal cord, controls breathing and heart rate. The basilar artery also sends branches to the cerebellum, which controls balance and helps coordinate movement. The basilar artery forms where the two vertebral arteries merge after they pass through the neck into the skull.
