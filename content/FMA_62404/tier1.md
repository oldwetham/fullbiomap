Each inferior colliculus is part of the brain's pathway for hearing. It passes sound signals to the thalamus, which relays them on so we can hear them. It sits on the back of the midbrain, the middle region of the brain. It belongs to the lower pair of four bumps called colliculi, Latin for “little hills.”
