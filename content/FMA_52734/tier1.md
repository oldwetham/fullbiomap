The frontal bone forms the forehead and the roof of each orbit, or eye socket. The frontal bone is also part of the brain case, which holds and protects the brain. Just behind the eyebrows, the frontal bone thickens into rounded brow ridges. Inside the frontal bone, above the eyebrows, is an air-filled space called the frontal sinus.
