Each digastric muscle lifts the hyoid bone, a U-shaped bone in the upper neck, when you swallow. The digastric also lifts the larynx, or voice box, during a swallow. Each digastric lies under the mandible, the bone that forms the lower jaw, and pulls the jaw down. Each digastric has two bellies, front and back; a belly is the bulky middle part of a muscle.
