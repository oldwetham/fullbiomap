Osteocytes are the main cells in mature bone, and they maintain its matrix, the nonliving material between cells. Each osteocyte sits in a space called a lacuna. Osteocytes send many extensions through channels called canaliculi to communicate and get nutrients. Osteoblasts, the cells that form new bone, become osteocytes once trapped in hardened matrix.
