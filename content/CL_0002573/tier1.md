A Schwann cell wraps around axons, the parts of nerve cells that carry electrical signals. Schwann cells insulate axons with myelin, a fatty layer that helps signals travel faster. Schwann cells work in the peripheral nervous system: every part of the nervous system except the brain and spinal cord. A Schwann cell is a glial cell, a cell that supports nerve cells.
