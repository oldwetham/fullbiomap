Each parotid gland makes saliva, a digestive fluid, and sends it into the mouth. The parotid glands are the biggest of the three pairs of salivary glands, and each sits in front of an ear. Saliva from the parotid gland is watery, mixes freely with food and starts the digestion of carbohydrates.
