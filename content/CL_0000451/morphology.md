# Dendritic cell: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Special structure:** class II MHC molecules  
  “Class II | Dendritic | Yes, in tissues | Brings antigens to regional lymph nodes” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 21.3 The Adaptive Immune Response: T lymphocytes and Their Functional Types, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/21-3-the-adaptive-immune-response-t-lymphocytes-and-their-functional-types)
- **Structure and job:** Living in tissues, it takes up and processes antigens and carries them to lymph nodes to activate T cells and start an immune response.  
  “A cell of hematopoietic origin, typically resident in particular tissues, specialized in the uptake, processing, and transport of antigens to lymph nodes for the purpose of stimulating an immune response via T cell activation.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000451 dendritic cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000451)
- **Structure and job:** It can engulf and kill pathogens by phagocytosis, but its main job is to carry their antigens to the regional draining lymph nodes.  
  “Dendritic cells also kill pathogens by phagocytosis (see ), but their major function is to bring antigens to regional draining lymph nodes.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 21.3 The Adaptive Immune Response: T lymphocytes and Their Functional Types, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/21-3-the-adaptive-immune-response-t-lymphocytes-and-their-functional-types)
- **Structure and job:** In the gut lining it opens the tight junctions between epithelial cells and pushes probes into the lumen to sample microbial antigens, then carries them to nearby lymphoid follicles for T cells to inspect.  
  “Dendritic cells open the tight junctions between epithelial cells and extend probes into the lumen to evaluate the microbial antigens. The dendritic cells with antigens then travel to neighboring lymphoid follicles in the mucosa where T cells inspect for antigens.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 23.5 The Small and Large Intestines, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-5-the-small-and-large-intestines)
- **Structure and job:** It processes antigen material and sits in the skin (as Langerhans cells) and in the linings of the nose, lungs, stomach and intestines.  
  “Dendritic cells are immune cells that process antigen material; they are present in the skin (Langerhans cells) and the lining of the nose, lungs, stomach, and intestines.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 42.2 Adaptive Immune Response, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/42-2-adaptive-immune-response)
- **Structure and job:** It is the main antigen-presenting cell of the mucosal immune system; B cells and macrophages play only minor roles there.  
  “APCs of the mucosal immune system are primarily dendritic cells, with B cells and macrophages having minor roles.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 42.2 Adaptive Immune Response, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/42-2-adaptive-immune-response)
