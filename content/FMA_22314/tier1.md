Each gluteus maximus pulls the thigh backward at the hip, a movement called extension. Of all the buttock muscles, the gluteus maximus lies closest to the surface. The gluteus maximus is also the largest of the gluteal muscles, the muscles that form the buttocks. These muscles rank among the most powerful in the body.
