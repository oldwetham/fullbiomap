The extensor carpi ulnaris extends the hand at the wrist, moving the hand backward. This muscle also adducts the hand, moving the hand toward the body's midline. The muscle runs from the humerus, the upper arm bone, along the back of the forearm. The muscle's other end attaches to the fifth metacarpal, a long bone of the palm.
