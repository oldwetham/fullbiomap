An erythrocyte, or red blood cell, carries oxygen from the lungs to the body's tissues. It also takes some carbon dioxide waste from the tissues back to the lungs. It is made in the bone marrow, the tissue inside bones, and circulates through the body. It is packed with hemoglobin, a compound that carries oxygen.
