The midbrain coordinates what sight, hearing and touch tell us about the space around us. The midbrain forms the top of the brain stem, which connects to the spinal cord. The midbrain has four bumps called colliculi, Latin for “little hills.” The upper pair, the superior colliculi, are involved in turning the eyes toward a sound or touch.
