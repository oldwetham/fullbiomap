Each cephalic vein drains blood from near the surface of the arm. The cephalic vein starts near the wrist and wraps around the forearm. The cephalic vein runs through the upper arm to the axillary vein, a major vein that drains the arm. In lean, toned arms, the cephalic vein is easy to see along the biceps muscle.
