Each flexor digitorum profundus flexes, or bends, the fingers and the hand at the wrist. This muscle lies in the deep anterior compartment, the group of deep muscles at the front of the forearm. The muscle ends in four tendons, strong bands that connect muscle to bone. These tendons run to the second, third, fourth and fifth fingers.
