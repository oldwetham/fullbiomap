The esophagus is a muscular tube that pushes swallowed food to the stomach. Waves of muscle contraction, called peristalsis, do the pushing. The esophagus begins at the pharynx, or throat, and stays collapsed when you are not swallowing. Along the way down, the esophagus lies behind the trachea, or windpipe.
