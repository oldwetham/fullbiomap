A sperm is the male gamete, a reproductive cell that contributes genetic material to form offspring. Sperm are made in the testes, inside tubes called seminiferous tubules. A sperm's tail is a flagellum, a long, hair-like part that moves the cell. The acrosome is a cap-like part of the sperm's head, holding enzymes that help the sperm get into the egg; enzymes are proteins that speed up chemical reactions.
