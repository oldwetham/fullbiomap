The hepatic portal vein carries nutrient-rich blood from the digestive tract to the liver. The liver processes this blood, removing certain wastes and storing extra nutrients for later use. The hepatic portal vein begins behind the pancreas, where two veins join. The hepatic portal vein enters the liver at the porta hepatis, the “gate to the liver.”
