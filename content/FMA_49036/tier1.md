The inferior rectus moves the eye down and toward the nose. The inferior rectus is one of six extraocular muscles, the muscles that move the eye. Inferior means below, and this muscle lies below the eyeball. This muscle runs from the back of the orbit, the bony eye socket, to the front part of the eyeball.
