The talus receives the body's weight from the tibia, or shin bone. The talus passes this weight backward to the calcaneus, or heel bone, and forward through the foot. Seven tarsal bones form the back half of the foot, and the talus is the highest. At the ankle joint, the talus meets the tibia and the fibula, a thin leg bone.
