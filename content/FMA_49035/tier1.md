Each superior rectus is an eye muscle that moves the eye up and toward the nose. To raise the eyes, the superior rectus muscles of both eyes contract. When the superior rectus raises the eye, the upper eyelid usually lifts too. The superior rectus runs from the back of the orbit, or eye socket, to the top of the eyeball.
