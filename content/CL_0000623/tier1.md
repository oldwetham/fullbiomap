Natural killer (NK) cells are white blood cells that kill cells infected with viruses. NK cells constantly patrol the body, helping to control infections. NK cells spot infected cells by changes in MHC I, a surface protein on all cells with a nucleus. NK cells kill infected cells by triggering apoptosis, or programmed cell death.
