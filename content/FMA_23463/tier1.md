Each radius joins two wrist bones to form the wrist joint. Rotating the radius turns the palm from facing forward to facing backward, a movement called pronation. Supination is the opposite: the radius rotates back, and the palm faces forward again. The forearm has two bones, and the radius is the one on the thumb side.
