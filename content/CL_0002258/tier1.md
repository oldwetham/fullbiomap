Thyroid follicular cells make thyroid hormones, chemical signals that control how the body works. These hormones set the body's basal metabolic rate, the energy the body needs at rest. Follicular cells form the walls of thyroid follicles, which hold a sticky fluid called colloid. To make thyroid hormones, follicular cells need iodide, a form of iodine that comes from the diet.
