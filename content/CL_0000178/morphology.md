# Leydig cell: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Special structure:** LH receptors  
  “In the testis, LH binds to LH receptors on Leydig cells and stimulates the release of testosterone.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 27.1 Anatomy and Physiology of the Testicular Reproductive System, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/27-1-anatomy-and-physiology-of-the-testicular-reproductive-system)
- **Structure and job:** It produces testosterone, an androgen and steroid hormone.  
  “Testosterone, an androgen, is a steroid hormone produced by Leydig cells.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 27.1 Anatomy and Physiology of the Testicular Reproductive System, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/27-1-anatomy-and-physiology-of-the-testicular-reproductive-system)
- **Structure and job:** Sitting in the interstitial area between the seminiferous tubules of the testis, it secretes testosterone.  
  “A Leydig cell is a testosterone-secreting cell in the interstitial area, between the seminiferous tubules, in the testis.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000178 Leydig cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000178)
- **Structure and job:** Receptors on the cell bind luteinizing hormone (LH), which steps up its testosterone production.  
  “LH binds to receptors on Leydig cells in the testes and upregulates the production of testosterone.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 27.1 Anatomy and Physiology of the Testicular Reproductive System, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/27-1-anatomy-and-physiology-of-the-testicular-reproductive-system)
- **Structure and job:** When stimulated by LH, it makes testosterone and releases it both into the testes and into the blood.  
  “LH also enters the testes and stimulates the interstitial cells of Leydig to make and release testosterone into the testes and the blood.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 43.4 Hormonal Control of Human Reproduction, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/43-4-hormonal-control-of-human-reproduction)
- **Structure and job:** In the embryonic testis, the testosterone it makes drives development of the male sexual organs.  
  “Testosterone produced by Leydig cells in the embryonic testis stimulates the development of male sexual organs.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 27.3 Development of the Male and Female Reproductive Systems, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/27-3-development-of-the-male-and-female-reproductive-systems)
- **Structure and job:** The testosterone it produces is also responsible for male secondary sexual characteristics.  
  “The Leydig cells produce testosterone, which also is responsible for the secondary sexual characteristics of males.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 43.4 Hormonal Control of Human Reproduction, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/43-4-hormonal-control-of-human-reproduction)
