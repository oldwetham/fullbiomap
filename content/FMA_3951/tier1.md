The subclavian arteries carry blood to the arms, chest, shoulders and back, and to the brain and spinal cord, which form the central nervous system. Each subclavian artery runs below the clavicle, or collarbone. The left subclavian artery branches directly off the arch of the aorta, the body's largest artery. But the right one branches off the brachiocephalic artery, the first branch of that arch.
