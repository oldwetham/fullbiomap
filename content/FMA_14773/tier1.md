The splenic artery brings blood into the spleen, where the blood is filtered. The splenic artery's four main branches supply the spleen, the stomach and the pancreas, a gland that releases digestive juices. The splenic artery branches off the celiac trunk, a major artery that supplies blood to the digestive organs.
