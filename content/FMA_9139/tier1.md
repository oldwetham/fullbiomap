Each thoracic vertebra is a spine bone that anchors ribs at the back of the chest. The twelve thoracic vertebrae, numbered T1 to T12, sit in the upper back. Thoracic vertebrae have facets, small flat spots where the ribs are attached. The thoracic vertebrae are part of the rib cage, which protects the heart and lungs.
