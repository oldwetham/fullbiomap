The cerebellum controls balance and helps you coordinate movements and learn new movement skills. The cerebellum sits at the base of the brain, on top of the brainstem, which joins the spinal cord. The cerebellum compares signals from the cerebrum, the brain's largest part, with sensory feedback from the body. If uneven ground or a strong wind throws off your walking, the cerebellum sends out a correcting command.
