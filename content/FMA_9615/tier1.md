The cricoid cartilage forms the lower part of the larynx, or voice box. The cricoid cartilage is a ring of cartilage, a firm but flexible tissue. The ring is wide at the back and thinner at the front. The ring sits below the thyroid cartilage, which forms the Adam's apple.
