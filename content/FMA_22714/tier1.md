Each longissimus capitis keeps the head upright and moves the head backward or to one side. Working alone, one longissimus capitis tilts and turns the head toward the muscle’s own side. This muscle reaches up from the spine to the mastoid process, a bony bump behind the earlobe. The longissimus capitis is part of the erector spinae, the back muscles that keep the body upright.
