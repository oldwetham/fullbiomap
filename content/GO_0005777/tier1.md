Like tiny sewage treatment plants, peroxisomes make harmful toxins safe. In liver cells, for example, they make alcohol harmless. Many of their reactions give off toxic hydrogen peroxide, which they safely break down into oxygen and water. Each peroxisome is small and round, with a single membrane around it.
