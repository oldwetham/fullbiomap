# Intestinal villus goblet cell: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Structure and job:** A goblet cell works as a one-cell gland: set between the columnar cells of a mucous membrane, it secretes mucus.  
  “a goblet cell is a mucous-secreting unicellular “gland” interspersed between the columnar epithelial cells of mucous membranes” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 4.2 Epithelial Tissue, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/4-2-epithelial-tissue)
- **Structure and job:** Scattered among the epithelial cells that line the gut, goblet cells release mucus and fluid into the lumen.  
  “Interspersed among its epithelial cells are goblet cells, which secrete mucus and fluid into the lumen” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 23.1 Overview of the Digestive System, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-1-overview-of-the-digestive-system)
