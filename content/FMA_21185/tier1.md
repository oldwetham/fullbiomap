The femoral vein carries blood away from the upper leg. Behind the knee, the popliteal vein, which drains that area, becomes the femoral vein. The femoral vein lies in the femoral triangle, the area where the hip meets the leg. After crossing the body wall, the femoral vein becomes the external iliac vein.
