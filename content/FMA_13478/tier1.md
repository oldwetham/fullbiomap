The vertebral column, or spine, supports the head and body and protects the spinal cord. The spine is a series of bones, called vertebrae, that runs from the skull to the tailbone. Pads called intervertebral discs sit between the vertebrae and let the spine move. The adult spine has four curves, which add strength and flexibility and help absorb shock.
