Each semispinalis thoracis extends, or bends back, the spine. This muscle also turns the spine toward the contralateral, or opposite, side. The semispinalis thoracis is a muscle of the thoracic region, the upper back. This muscle attaches to spinous processes, the bumps you can feel down the middle of the back.
