The spinal cord carries messages both ways between the brain and the body. The spinal cord also controls reflexes, quick movements made without thinking, like pulling a hand away from a hot object. The spinal cord lies inside the spine, which surrounds and protects the cord. Together, the brain and the spinal cord make up the central nervous system.
