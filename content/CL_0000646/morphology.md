# Basal cell: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Special structure:** keratin (cytokeratin intermediate filaments)  
  “contain cytokeratin intermediate filament” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000646 basal cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000646)
- **Structure and job:** Undifferentiated, with little cytoplasm and few organelles, the basal cell is a stem cell that divides by mitosis to give rise to other epithelial cell types.  
  “Undifferentiated; mitotic stem cell for other epithelial cell types; rounded or elliptical with little cytoplasm and few organelles” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000646 basal cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000646)
- **Structure and job:** In the epidermis, basal cells form a single layer of stem cells that constantly divide by mitosis, and every keratinocyte comes from them.  
  “A basal cell is a cuboidal-shaped stem cell that is a precursor of the keratinocytes of the epidermis. All of the keratinocytes are produced from this single layer of cells, which are constantly going through mitosis to produce new cells.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 5.1 Layers of the Skin, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/5-1-layers-of-the-skin)
