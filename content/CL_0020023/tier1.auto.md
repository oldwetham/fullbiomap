A metabolically highly active chondrocyte that is identified in the cartilaginous tissues, including articular cartilage and intervertebral disc cartilage. In the human articular cartilage, this cell is marked by genes such as CYTL1, FRZB, CLEC3A, and S100B (Ji et al., 2018; Raut et al.,2025). This cell demonstrates strong metabolic activity and is implicated in extracellular matrix regulation and adaptive responses (Ji et al., 2018; Gan et al., 2021; Raut et al.,2025).
