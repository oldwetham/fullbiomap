Podocytes wrap finger-like arms around capillaries, the smallest blood vessels, to help form a filter. Podocytes are kidney cells inside a glomerulus, a tuft of capillaries that filters blood. Some plasma, the liquid part of blood, filters out through small gaps between the arms. But blood cells and medium-to-large proteins cannot pass between the arms.
