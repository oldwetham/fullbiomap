Together, the splenius muscles extend, or bend back, the head and neck. The splenius muscles can also turn the head and tilt it to the side. The splenius muscles, the splenius capitis and splenius cervicis, lie at the back of the neck. Each splenius muscle starts at the midline and runs upward and outward.
