Each palatine bone helps form the hard palate, the bony roof of the mouth. Together, the right and left palatine bones form the back quarter of the hard palate. Each palatine bone lies at the back of the nasal cavity, the inside of the nose. Each palatine bone also forms a small part of the floor of the orbit, or eye socket.
