The fibularis brevis plantar flexes the foot at the ankle, pointing the toes downward. This muscle also everts the foot, turning the bottom of the foot away from the midline. The fibularis brevis lies on the outer side of the leg, between the knee and the ankle. Another name for the fibularis brevis is the peroneus brevis.
