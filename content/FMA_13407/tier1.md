Each sternocleidomastoid is a major muscle that turns the head and tilts it to the side. Working alone, one sternocleidomastoid turns the head toward the opposite side, but both together bend the head forward. Each muscle runs from the breastbone and collarbone to the mastoid process, a bony bump just behind the earlobe. The sternocleidomastoid divides the neck into front and back triangles.
