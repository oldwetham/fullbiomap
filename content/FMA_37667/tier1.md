Each brachialis adds power when you flex the forearm, or bend the elbow. The brachialis lies beneath the biceps brachii, a muscle at the front of the upper arm. The brachialis starts on the front of the humerus, the upper arm bone. When you lift a cup, the brachialis is a synergist, or helper, to the biceps brachii.
