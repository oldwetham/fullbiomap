The anterior spinal artery carries blood to the anterior, or front, part of the spinal cord. This artery runs along the front surface of the spinal cord. The vertebral arteries pass through the neck and enter the skull. Branches from the left and right vertebral arteries merge to form the anterior spinal artery.
