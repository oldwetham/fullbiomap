Dense granules are tiny sacs, wrapped in a membrane, that store chemicals and release them. These sacs sit inside platelets, small bits of cells that form a plug at wounds to stop bleeding. Dense granules store ADP, which helps more platelets stick at a wound and makes the plug bigger. Dense granules also store serotonin, which keeps blood vessels narrowed.
