The teres minor turns the arm outward at the shoulder, a motion called lateral rotation. The teres minor also extends the arm, moving the arm backward. The teres minor starts on the outer edge of the scapula, or shoulder blade. The teres minor is one of four rotator cuff muscles, whose tendons circle and support the shoulder.
