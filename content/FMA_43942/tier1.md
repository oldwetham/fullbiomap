The plantar arch is an arch of arteries that brings blood to the toes and the rest of the foot. An artery is a blood vessel that carries blood away from the heart. The plantar arch forms where the plantar arteries join the dorsalis pedis artery, an artery of the foot. A place where blood vessels join like this is called an anastomosis.
