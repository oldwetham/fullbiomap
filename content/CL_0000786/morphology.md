# Plasma cell: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Organelles:** rough ER: 3 abundant  
  “These cells differ in morphology from standard B and T cells in that they contain a large amount of cytoplasm packed with the protein-synthesizing machinery known as rough endoplasmic reticulum.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 21.1 Anatomy of the Lymphatic and Immune Systems, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/21-1-anatomy-of-the-lymphatic-and-immune-systems)
- **Special structure:** no antibodies on its surface (a naive B cell carries surface antibody; the plasma cell does not)  
  “distinguishes a naïve B cell with antibody on its surface from an antibody-secreting plasma cell with no antibodies on its surface.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 21.4 The Adaptive Immune Response: B-lymphocytes and Antibodies, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/21-4-the-adaptive-immune-response-b-lymphocytes-and-antibodies)
- **Special structure:** secreted soluble antibodies  
  “A plasma cell is a B cell that has differentiated in response to antigen binding, and has thereby gained the ability to secrete soluble antibodies.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 21.1 Anatomy of the Lymphatic and Immune Systems, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/21-1-anatomy-of-the-lymphatic-and-immune-systems)
- **Special structure:** IgM pentamers (five antibodies linked by a joining (J) chain), assembled before secretion  
  “Prior to antibody secretion, plasma cells assemble IgM molecules into pentamers (five individual antibodies) linked by a joining (J) chain” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 42.3 Antibodies, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/42-3-antibodies)
