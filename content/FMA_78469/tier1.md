The fourth ventricle is a diamond-shaped space that holds cerebrospinal fluid, a clear cushioning liquid. This ventricle lies in the region of the brain stem, the brain's link to the spinal cord. Fluid arrives through the cerebral aqueduct, a narrow channel, and the fourth ventricle adds more. Openings in the fourth ventricle then let the fluid flow out around the brain and spinal cord.
