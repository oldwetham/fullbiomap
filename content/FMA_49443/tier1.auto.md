Postnatal anatomical structure, each instance of which has as it direct parts a heterogeneous collection of some instances of two or more subclasses of anatomcial structure, which are spatially related to one another; the collections as a whole does not satisfy the definition of any other subclass of anatomical structure. Eaxmples: root of lung, back, anatomical snuffbox, motor unit, juxtaglomerular complex.
