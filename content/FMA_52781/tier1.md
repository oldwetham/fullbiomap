The external ear collects sound waves and sends them through the ear canal to the eardrum. On the side of the head, the external ear includes the ear canal and the auricle, the fleshy outer part of the ear. Cartilage, a firm but bendable tissue, gives the external ear flexible strength and support. Sound from one spot reaches the two auricles at slightly different times, which lets the brain tell where the sound came from.
