Ependymal cells filter blood to make cerebrospinal fluid (CSF), a clear liquid around the brain and spinal cord. These cells line the ventricles, the open spaces inside the brain where CSF flows. Ependymal cells also line the central canal, a hollow space inside the spinal cord. Ependymal cells have cilia, short hair-like structures that help move CSF through the ventricles.
