A macrophage is a large cell that engulfs foreign particles and pathogens, agents that can cause disease. It also cleans up dead and damaged cells in the body. Macrophages live in many tissues of the body; some roam freely, while others stay fixed in place. They are monocytes, a type of white blood cell, that have left the bloodstream.
