Each eye detects light, which gives us the sense of sight. Each eye sits in a bony socket of the skull called the orbit, which protects the eyeball. The retina, a layer of light-sensing cells at the back of the eye, changes light into nerve signals. The clear cornea at the front of the eye and the lens bend light to focus an image on the retina.
