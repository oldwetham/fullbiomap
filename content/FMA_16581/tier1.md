The pelvic girdle attaches a lower limb to the axial skeleton, the bones of the head and trunk. The pelvic girdle, also called the hip girdle, is formed by a single bone, the hip bone. Built to steady and support the body, the pelvic girdle moves very little. Most muscles that attach to and move the femur, or thigh bone, start on the pelvic girdle.
