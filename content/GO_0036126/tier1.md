The flagellum is a long, hair-like structure that moves an entire sperm cell. In humans, only the sperm cell has a flagellum, which propels the sperm toward an egg. Mitochondria, the cell's energy makers, power the flagellum, which runs through the sperm's tail. The flagellum is built from microtubules, which are small hollow tubes made of protein.
