Each putamen helps control movement and lies deep inside the cerebrum, the brain's largest part. The putamen belongs to the basal nuclei, groups of brain cells involved in movement and motivation. With the caudate, another basal nucleus, the putamen forms the striatum. The putamen and the globus pallidus, a layered nucleus, fit together like curved lenses.
