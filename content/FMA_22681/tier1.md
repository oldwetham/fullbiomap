Each splenius cervicis helps the head tip back, tilt to the side and turn. The splenius cervicis and the splenius capitis are the two splenius muscles at the back of the neck. The splenius muscles start at the midline and run upward and out to the sides. The splenius cervicis ends in the cervical region, the part of the spine in the neck.
