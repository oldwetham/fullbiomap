The coracobrachialis flexes the arm, moving the arm forward at the shoulder. This muscle also adducts the arm, bringing the arm down to the body's side. The coracobrachialis starts on the scapula, or shoulder blade. The muscle's other end attaches to the humerus, the upper arm bone.
