The zona pellucida is a coat that protects the egg. This clear coat wraps around the egg, under an outer layer of cells. A sperm must get through the zona pellucida before fertilization can happen. After fertilization, the egg releases material into the zona pellucida, creating a barrier that blocks more sperm.
