Each internal capsule is part of the descending motor pathway, which carries movement commands from the brain to the spinal cord. The internal capsule also holds nerve fibers that carry signals from the senses. It passes between the caudate nucleus and putamen, deep parts of the brain that control movement. The internal capsule is white matter, tissue made mostly of nerve fibers with a fatty coating.
