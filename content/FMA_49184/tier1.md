The mouth takes in food and starts breaking it down. The lips, cheeks and palate form the borders of the mouth, with the palate as the roof. Saliva, a watery fluid that salivary glands release into the mouth, moistens food and begins the breakdown of carbohydrates. Chewing and tongue movements break food into small bits and mix the bits with saliva.
