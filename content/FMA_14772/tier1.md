The hepatic artery proper supplies the liver with systemic blood, oxygen-rich blood sent out from the heart. This artery branches off the common hepatic artery, which also feeds parts of the stomach. The hepatic artery proper then gives rise to the left hepatic artery, which is part of the liver's left lobe. The hepatic artery proper also gives rise to the right hepatic artery, which is part of the right lobe.
