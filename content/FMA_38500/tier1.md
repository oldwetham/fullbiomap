Each extensor digitorum extends, or straightens, the fingers and the hand at the wrist. This muscle lies near the surface at the back of the forearm, between the elbow and the wrist. The extensor digitorum starts on the humerus, the bone of the upper arm. The muscle's other end attaches to the phalanges, the bones of the fingers.
