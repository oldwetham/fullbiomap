The diencephalon connects the cerebrum, the brain's largest part, with the rest of the nervous system. The diencephalon sits in the center of the brain, almost surrounded by the cerebrum. The thalamus, one of the diencephalon's two main parts, relays signals from the senses. The other is the hypothalamus, which keeps body temperature, eating and drinking at the right levels.
