The hemiazygos vein drains blood from the chest, along the left side of the backbone. The hemiazygos vein is a smaller partner of the azygos vein, which runs up the backbone's right side. The hemiazygos vein collects blood from the left intercostal veins, which drain the chest wall muscles. The hemiazygos vein also drains blood from the esophagus, the tube that connects the mouth to the stomach.
