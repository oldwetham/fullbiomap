# Ependymal cell: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Shape:** other: epithelial-like cell in a single, tightly joined layer  
  “These glial cells appear similar to epithelial cells, making a single layer of cells with little intracellular space and tight connections between adjacent cells.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 12.2 Nervous Tissue, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/12-2-nervous-tissue)
- **Special structure:** tight connections between adjacent cells  
  “These glial cells appear similar to epithelial cells, making a single layer of cells with little intracellular space and tight connections between adjacent cells.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 12.2 Nervous Tissue, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/12-2-nervous-tissue)
- **Structure and job:** Cilia on the cell's apical surface help move cerebrospinal fluid through the ventricles.  
  “They also have cilia on their apical surface to help move the CSF through the ventricular space.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 12.2 Nervous Tissue, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/12-2-nervous-tissue)
- **Structure and job:** At the choroid plexus, ependymal cells contact blood vessels and filter and absorb components of the blood to produce cerebrospinal fluid.  
  “The choroid plexus is a specialized structure in the ventricles where ependymal cells come in contact with blood vessels and filter and absorb components of the blood to produce cerebrospinal fluid.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 12.2 Nervous Tissue, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/12-2-nervous-tissue)
- **Structure and job:** Because they contact blood vessels and filter blood components to make cerebrospinal fluid, ependymal cells can be counted as part of the blood-brain barrier, or as a place where that barrier breaks down.  
  “ependymal cells come in contact with blood vessels and filter and absorb components of the blood to produce cerebrospinal fluid. Because of this, ependymal cells can be considered a component of the BBB, or a place where the BBB breaks down.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 12.2 Nervous Tissue, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/12-2-nervous-tissue)
- **Structure and job:** Ependymal cells surround blood capillaries and filter the blood to make cerebrospinal fluid.  
  “Ependymal cells (one of the types of glial cells described in the introduction to the nervous system) surround blood capillaries and filter the blood to make CSF.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 13.3 Circulation and the Central Nervous System, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/13-3-circulation-and-the-central-nervous-system)
- **Structure and job:** Lining the fluid-filled ventricles of the brain and the central canal of the spinal cord, ependymal cells help produce the cerebrospinal fluid that cushions the brain.  
  “Ependymal cells line fluid-filled ventricles of the brain and the central canal of the spinal cord. They are involved in the production of cerebrospinal fluid, which serves as a cushion for the brain” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 35.1 Neurons and Glial Cells, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/35-1-neurons-and-glial-cells)
