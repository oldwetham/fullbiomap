The cell membrane, also called the plasma membrane, separates the inside of a cell from the outside. The membrane forms a protective barrier around the cell and controls what passes in or out. Two layers of phospholipids, molecules with a head and two tails, make up the membrane. Membrane proteins form channels that let materials through and act as markers that identify the cell.
