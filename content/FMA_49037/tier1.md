The medial rectus moves the eye toward the nose. The medial rectus is one of six extraocular muscles, the muscles that move the eye. Medial means toward the middle, and this muscle attaches to the side of the eyeball nearest the nose. To look at something close to the face, both medial rectus muscles turn the eyes toward the nose.
