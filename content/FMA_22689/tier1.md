Each brachial artery supplies blood to much of the upper arm. Smaller branches of the brachial artery supply the back of the arm and the elbow. This artery continues from the axillary artery, which supplies the armpit. The brachial artery then splits into the radial and ulnar arteries, which continue into the forearm.
