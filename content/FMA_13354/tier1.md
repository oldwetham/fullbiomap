The intercostal muscles help you breathe by changing the size of the rib cage. The intercostal muscles are three sets of muscles that stretch across the spaces between the ribs. The outer set, the external intercostals, raises the rib cage to help you breathe in. The deeper sets, however, pull the ribs together to help you breathe out.
