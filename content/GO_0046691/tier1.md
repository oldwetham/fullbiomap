Intracellular canaliculi are a network of channels for releasing substances from parietal cells, the stomach cells that make acid. Each narrow channel forms where the cell membrane, which wraps around the cell, folds inward. These channels seem to reach into the cell, but they are part of the cell membrane. Many microvilli line each channel; these projections add to a cell's surface area.
