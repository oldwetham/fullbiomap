Each anterior tibial vein collects blood from around the tibialis anterior, a muscle at the front of the lower leg. This vein starts at the dorsal venous arch, which drains blood from the top of the foot. The anterior tibial vein runs alongside the anterior tibial artery, which feeds the front of the lower leg and the foot. This vein empties into the popliteal vein, which drains the area behind the knee.
