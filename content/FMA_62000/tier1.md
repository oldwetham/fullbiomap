The telencephalon becomes the cerebrum, the part of the brain in charge of memory, emotion and consciousness. Of all the parts of the brain, the cerebrum is the largest. A large gap splits the cerebrum into right and left halves, called cerebral hemispheres. As the telencephalon grows inside the skull, the brain forms folds that look alike in everyone.
