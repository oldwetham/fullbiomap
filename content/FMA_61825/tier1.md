Each temporal lobe mainly processes sounds and makes sense of them. Parts of the temporal lobe also help with speech, memory and emotions. The temporal lobe sits at the base of the brain, near the ears. Each half of the cerebral cortex, the brain's folded outer layer, has a temporal lobe.
