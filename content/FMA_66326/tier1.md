Each pulmonary artery carries blood that is low in oxygen from the heart to one lung. The pulmonary trunk, a large vessel that leaves the heart, splits to form both pulmonary arteries. In the lung, each artery branches many times and leads to capillaries, the smallest blood vessels. These capillaries wrap around alveoli, tiny air sacs where oxygen and carbon dioxide are exchanged.
