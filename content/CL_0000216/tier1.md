A Sertoli cell surrounds and nourishes germ cells, the developing sperm cells. Sertoli cells sit inside the seminiferous tubules, where the testis makes sperm. Sertoli cells are joined to each other by tight junctions, firm seals between cells. These junctions form the blood–testis barrier, which keeps substances in the blood from reaching germ cells.
