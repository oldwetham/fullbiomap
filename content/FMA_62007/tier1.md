The thalamus relays signals from the senses to the cerebral cortex, the brain's outer layer. Signals from every sense except smell pass through the thalamus before reaching the cortex. The thalamus also helps control sleep and how awake and aware we are. The thalamus is made of two oval masses near the center of the brain.
