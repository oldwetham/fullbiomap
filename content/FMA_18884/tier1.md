Each internal iliac vein drains blood from the pelvic organs and the inner thigh. Several smaller veins in the pelvis join to form this vein. This vein runs beside the internal iliac artery, which supplies the organs and walls of the pelvis. Each internal iliac vein then joins the external iliac vein, which drains the leg, to form the common iliac vein.
