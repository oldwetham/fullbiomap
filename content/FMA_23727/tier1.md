Each capitate is a carpal bone, one of the eight small bones that form the wrist and the base of the hand. The capitate is the largest of the carpal bones and sits in the center of the hand. The capitate joins the third metacarpal, the palm bone that leads to the middle finger. The name capitate means “head-shaped.”
