The nucleus is the cell's control center because it stores the genetic instructions for making proteins. These instructions are kept in DNA, the cell's genetic material. The nucleus is wrapped in the nuclear envelope, a double membrane with pores that let materials in and out. Red blood cells have no nucleus, because they push it out as they mature.
