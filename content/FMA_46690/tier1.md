Each genioglossus muscle pulls the tongue down and forward. The genioglossus lets you stick your tongue out. The fan-shaped genioglossus runs from the mandible, or lower jaw bone, to the underside of the tongue. The genioglossus is an extrinsic tongue muscle, which means it starts outside the tongue but attaches inside the tongue.
