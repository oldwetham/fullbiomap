Intermediate filaments help cells resist tension, the forces that pull cells apart. Each filament is made of long protein strands wound together like a rope. Several proteins can form these strands, and the best known is keratin, which strengthens hair, nails and skin. These filaments also hold parts of the cell in place and link neighboring cells together.
