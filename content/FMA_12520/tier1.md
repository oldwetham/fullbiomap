The axis acts as the pivot when you turn your head to the right or left. The axis is the second of the seven cervical vertebrae, the bones of the spine in the neck. The axis looks much like typical cervical vertebrae, but it has the dens, a bony part that sticks up. The atlas, the first cervical vertebra, turns around the dens when you shake your head “no.”
