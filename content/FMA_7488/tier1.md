The xiphoid process is where the diaphragm, the main breathing muscle, attaches at the front. The xiphoid process is the small tip at the bottom of the sternum, or breastbone. Early in life, the xiphoid process is made of cartilage, a tough but flexible tissue. But starting in middle age, the xiphoid process slowly turns into bone.
