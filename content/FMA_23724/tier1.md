The trapezoid is a small carpal bone that helps form the wrist and the base of the hand. Carpal bones give stability and support while allowing some limited motion. The trapezoid lies between the trapezium and the capitate, which are also carpal bones. The trapezoid also forms a joint with the palm bone that connects to the index finger.
