# Purkinje myocyte: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Shape:** other: short, wide cylinder (shorter than ordinary cardiac myocytes but nearly twice their diameter; cells joined end to end in long rows)  
  “They are uninucleate cylindrical cells, associated end-to-end in long rows, continue from the node to the atrioventricular bundle; relatively short compared to ordinary myocytes but are nearly twice their diameter.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0002068 Purkinje myocyte, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0002068)
- **Nuclei:** 1  
  “They are uninucleate cylindrical cells” (Cell Ontology, CL:0002068 Purkinje myocyte, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0002068)
- **Structure and job:** Like other myocardial conducting cells, they are specialized muscle cells that work much like neurons, propagating the action potential that triggers the heart's contractions.  
  “Their function is similar in many respects to neurons, although they are specialized muscle cells. Myocardial conduction cells initiate and propagate the action potential (the electrical impulse) that travels throughout the heart and triggers the contractions that propel the blood.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 19.2 Cardiac Muscle and Electrical Activity, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/19-2-cardiac-muscle-and-electrical-activity)
- **Structure and job:** Purkinje fibers have an inherent firing rate of about 15–20 impulses per minute, slower than the 20–30 of the bundle branches.  
  “The bundle branches would have an inherent rate of 20–30 impulses per minute, and the Purkinje fibers would fire at 15–20 impulses per minute.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 19.2 Cardiac Muscle and Electrical Activity, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/19-2-cardiac-muscle-and-electrical-activity)
