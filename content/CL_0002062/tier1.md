A pulmonary alveolar type 1 cell forms the thin barrier between air and blood that gas exchange needs. In gas exchange, oxygen passes into the blood and carbon dioxide passes out. Type 1 cells are flat and cover almost all of the surface of the alveoli, small sacs in the lungs.
