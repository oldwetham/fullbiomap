Each thyrohyoid pulls down the hyoid bone, a small U-shaped bone in the upper neck. When you swallow or speak, each thyrohyoid also lifts the larynx, or voice box. This front neck muscle runs from the thyroid cartilage, or Adam's apple, to the hyoid bone.
