The infrahyoid muscles pull down the hyoid bone, a U-shaped bone in the upper neck. Their pull on the hyoid bone helps with swallowing and speech. These strap-like muscles also control the position of the larynx, or voice box. They attach to the hyoid bone from below, in the front of the neck.
