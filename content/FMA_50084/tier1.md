The posterior communicating artery helps supply blood to the brain. There is one on each side of the base of the brain. Each one links the posterior cerebral artery to the internal carotid artery, a main source of blood for the brain. In this way, each one forms part of the circle of Willis, a group of blood vessels around the base of the brain.
