# Melanocyte: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Granules:** melanin-filled pigment granules; look: brown to black; abundance: unknown; position: unknown  
  “Contains melanin-filled pigment granules, which gives a brown to black appearance.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000148 melanocyte, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000148)
- **Structure and job:** Melanocytes make the pigment melanin, which colors skin and hair and helps shield the living cells of the epidermis from UV radiation damage.  
  “The second is a melanocyte, a cell that produces the pigment melanin. Melanin gives hair and skin its color, and also helps protect the living cells of the epidermis from ultraviolet (UV) radiation damage.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 5.1 Layers of the Skin, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/5-1-layers-of-the-skin)
- **Structure and job:** UV exposure stimulates melanocytes to make and secrete more melanin.  
  “Exposure to UV irradiation stimulates the melanocytes to produce and secrete more melanin.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 5.1 Layers of the Skin, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/5-1-layers-of-the-skin)
- **Structure and job:** Sun-exposed keratinocytes release chemicals that stimulate melanocytes, so more melanin is made and builds up in the keratinocytes.  
  “Exposure to the UV rays of the sun or a tanning salon causes melanin to be manufactured and built up in keratinocytes, as sun exposure stimulates keratinocytes to secrete chemicals that stimulate melanocytes.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 5.1 Layers of the Skin, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/5-1-layers-of-the-skin)
- **Structure and job:** Melanocytes in the hair papilla make the melanin that colors hair.  
  “hair gets its color from the pigment melanin, produced by melanocytes in the hair papilla.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 5.2 Accessory Structures of the Skin, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/5-2-accessory-structures-of-the-skin)
- **Structure and job:** Melanocyte-stimulating hormone acts on skin melanocytes to increase their melanin production.  
  “melanocyte-stimulating hormone (MSH) | stimulates melanocytes of the skin increasing melanin pigment production.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 37.5 Endocrine Glands, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/37-5-endocrine-glands)
