The small cardiac vein drains blood from the back surfaces of the heart's right side. This vein runs beside the right coronary artery, a vessel that supplies blood to the heart. The small cardiac vein lies in the coronary sulcus, a deep, fat-filled groove on the heart's surface. The small cardiac vein empties into the coronary sinus, a large vein on the back of the heart.
