Each renal artery carries blood to a kidney, an organ that filters the blood. The right and left renal arteries branch off the aorta, the largest artery in the body. The right renal artery is longer than the left, because the aorta lies to the left of the spine. Each renal artery enters its kidney at the hilum, the curved-in part of the kidney's bean shape.
