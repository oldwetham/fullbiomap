The inferior vena cava is a large vein that returns blood from the lower body to the heart. Blood from the legs, abdomen and pelvis flows into this vein. In the abdomen, the inferior vena cava runs alongside the aorta, the body's largest artery. The inferior vena cava empties into the right atrium, an upper chamber of the heart.
