Each anterior cerebral artery feeds the frontal lobe, the front part of the brain above the eyes. This artery is part of the circle of Willis, a ring of arteries at the base of the brain. This artery branches off the internal carotid artery, a main source of blood for the brain. The right and left anterior cerebral arteries are joined by the anterior communicating artery.
