The aorta is the body's largest artery, a blood vessel that carries blood away from the heart. Its branches carry blood to almost every organ of the body. It receives oxygen-rich blood from the left ventricle, the main pumping chamber on the heart's lower left side. It rises from the heart, curves to the left, then runs down through the chest and abdomen.
