The thyroid cartilage forms the front of the larynx, or voice box. The thyroid cartilage is the larynx's largest piece of cartilage, a firm but flexible tissue. Two plates of the thyroid cartilage join to form a bump called the Adam's apple. The vocal cords, the folds that vibrate to make sound, are attached to the thyroid cartilage.
