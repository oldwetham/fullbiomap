Pseudopodia are temporary extensions of a cell that help it move and feed. In the body, white blood cells called macrophages use pseudopodia to move through tissues. Pseudopodia also let macrophages squeeze through the walls of capillaries, the smallest blood vessels. Cytoplasm, a cell's inner material, flows into a pseudopod, and the whole cell follows.
