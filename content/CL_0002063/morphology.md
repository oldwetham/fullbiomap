# Pulmonary alveolar type 2 cell: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Shape:** cube  
  “This cuboidal cell is thicker than squamous alveolar cells, has a rounded apical surface that projects above the level of the surrounding epithelium” (Cell Ontology, CL:0002063 pulmonary alveolar type 2 cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0002063)
- **Surface:** microvilli  
  “its free surface is covered by short microvilli” (Cell Ontology, CL:0002063 pulmonary alveolar type 2 cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0002063)
- **Special structure:** alveolar lamellar bodies  
  “secreting and recycling surfactants via specialized organelles called alveolar lamellar bodies” (Cell Ontology, CL:0002063 pulmonary alveolar type 2 cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0002063)
- **Structure and job:** Its lamellar bodies let it secrete and recycle surfactant, which is how it modulates the fluid around the alveolar epithelium.  
  “A pulmonary alveolar epithelial cell that modulates the fluid surrounding the alveolar epithelium by secreting and recycling surfactants via specialized organelles called alveolar lamellar bodies.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0002063 pulmonary alveolar type 2 cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0002063)
- **Structure and job:** Scattered among the type I cells, it secretes pulmonary surfactant, a mix of phospholipids and proteins that lowers surface tension in the alveoli.  
  “A type II alveolar cell is interspersed among the type I cells and secretes pulmonary surfactant, a substance composed of phospholipids and proteins that reduces the surface tension of the alveoli.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 22.1 Organs and Structures of the Respiratory System, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/22-1-organs-and-structures-of-the-respiratory-system)
- **Structure and job:** The surfactant it secretes reduces surface tension, which keeps the alveoli from collapsing during expiration.  
  “However, pulmonary surfactant secreted by type II alveolar cells mixes with that water and helps reduce this surface tension. Without pulmonary surfactant, the alveoli would collapse during expiration.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 22.3 The Process of Breathing, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/22-3-the-process-of-breathing)
- **Structure and job:** It also takes part in tissue repair, and after injury it can differentiate into a type 1 alveolar cell.  
  “This cell type also contributes to tissue repair and can differentiate after injury into a pulmonary alveolar type 1 cell.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0002063 pulmonary alveolar type 2 cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0002063)
