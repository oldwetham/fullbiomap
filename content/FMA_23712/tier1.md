Each lunate helps form the wrist joint with the radius, the forearm bone on the thumb side. At this joint, the rounded lunate meets a shallow hollow at the end of the radius. The lunate is one of eight carpal bones, the small bones of the wrist. The name lunate means “moon-shaped,” and this bone sits between the scaphoid and triquetrum bones.
