The lateral rectus moves the eye away from the nose. The lateral rectus is one of six extraocular muscles, the muscles that move the eye. Lateral means toward the side, and this muscle lies on the outer side of the eyeball. The abducens nerve, one of the cranial nerves connected to the brain, controls this muscle.
