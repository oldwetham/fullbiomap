The bronchial arteries bring oxygen-rich blood to the lungs. This supply comes in addition to the blood pumped to the lungs to pick up oxygen. Like vessels elsewhere in the body, the bronchial arteries feed the lung tissue with nutrients. In the chest, the bronchial arteries branch off the aorta, the body's largest artery.
