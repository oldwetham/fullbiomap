Each ovary makes eggs, called oocytes, and sex hormones such as estrogen and progesterone. The ovaries sit in the pelvis, one on each side of the uterus. Each oocyte grows with supporting cells around it, and this group is called a follicle. About every 28 days, one follicle releases its oocyte in a process called ovulation.
