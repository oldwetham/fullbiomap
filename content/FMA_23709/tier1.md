Each scaphoid helps form the wrist joint with the radius, the forearm bone on the thumb side. The scaphoid also helps anchor a strong ligament that holds the wrist bones in a U-shaped group. The scaphoid is one of eight carpal bones, the small bones of the wrist. The name scaphoid means “boat-shaped,” and this bone sits on the thumb side of the wrist.
