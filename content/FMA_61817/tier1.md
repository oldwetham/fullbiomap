Each cerebral hemisphere controls the opposite side of the body. A deep groove divides the cerebrum, the largest part of the brain, into two cerebral hemispheres. The left hemisphere is more devoted to language, and the right to spatial and nonverbal reasoning. However, neither hemisphere has these functions all to itself.
