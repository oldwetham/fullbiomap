The patella, or kneecap, protects a large thigh muscle's tendon from rubbing on the thigh bone. It is the largest sesamoid bone in the body, a bone that sits inside a tendon. It lies in front of the knee and forms a joint with the thigh bone. It also lifts the tendon away from the knee joint, which gives the muscle more leverage.
