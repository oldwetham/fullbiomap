Each semispinalis cervicis moves the second to fifth cervical vertebrae, the neck bones of the spine. The movement is extension: bending this part of the spine backward or bringing it back upright. From those neck bones, this muscle runs down to the upper thoracic vertebrae, in the upper back. This muscle lies under the semispinalis capitis, which sits in the upper neck.
