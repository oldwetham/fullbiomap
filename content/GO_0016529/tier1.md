The sarcoplasmic reticulum stores calcium that a muscle cell needs to contract. The sarcoplasmic reticulum is a fine network of membranes that runs all through the cell. An electrical signal makes the sarcoplasmic reticulum release calcium, which starts the contraction. Pumps then move the calcium back into storage, so the cell relaxes.
