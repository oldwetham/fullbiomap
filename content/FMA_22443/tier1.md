The adductor magnus adducts the thigh, pulling the thigh toward the body's midline. The front part of this muscle also flexes the thigh, bringing the thigh forward and upward. But the back part of the adductor magnus helps extend the thigh, moving the thigh backward. This muscle lies on the medial side of the thigh, the side toward the body's middle.
