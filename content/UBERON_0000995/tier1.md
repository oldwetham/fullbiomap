The uterus is a hollow, muscular organ where a fetus, a developing human, grows during pregnancy. The uterus sits in the pelvis, with an ovary on each side. Part of the endometrium, the inner lining of the uterus, is shed during each menstrual period and then grows back. During labor, the muscle of the uterus contracts to help push the baby out.
