# Perineuronal satellite cell: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Nucleus shape:** round  
  “Also, the small round nuclei of satellite cells can be seen surrounding—as if they were orbiting—the neuron cell bodies.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 13.4 The Peripheral Nervous System, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/13-4-the-peripheral-nervous-system)
- **Nucleus size:** small  
  “Also, the small round nuclei of satellite cells can be seen surrounding—as if they were orbiting—the neuron cell bodies.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 13.4 The Peripheral Nervous System, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/13-4-the-peripheral-nervous-system)
- **Structure and job:** Satellite cells surround the cell bodies of neurons in sensory and autonomic ganglia and support them, playing a role in the periphery similar to the one astrocytes play in the CNS.  
  “Satellite cells are found in sensory and autonomic ganglia, where they surround the cell bodies of neurons. This accounts for the name, based on their appearance under the microscope. They provide support, performing similar functions in the periphery as astrocytes do in the CNS” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 12.2 Nervous Tissue, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/12-2-nervous-tissue)
- **Structure and job:** Satellite glia nourish the neurons of the peripheral nervous system and give them structural support.  
  “Satellite glia provide nutrients and structural support for neurons in the PNS.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 35.1 Neurons and Glial Cells, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/35-1-neurons-and-glial-cells)
