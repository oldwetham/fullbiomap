Each precentral gyrus is the primary motor cortex, the area that starts movements. Its cells tell the spinal cord to move skeletal muscles, which attach to bones. Each precentral gyrus controls movement on the opposite side of the body. The precentral gyrus is the rearmost part of the frontal lobe, the brain's front region.
