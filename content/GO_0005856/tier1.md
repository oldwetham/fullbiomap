The cytoskeleton is a network of protein fibers that maintains a cell's shape. These fibers also help a cell move, reproduce and carry substances around inside it. The fibers run throughout the cell and come in three kinds. From thinnest to thickest, the three kinds are microfilaments, intermediate filaments and microtubules.
