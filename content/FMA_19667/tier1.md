The urethra carries urine from the bladder to the outside of the body. Two rings of muscle, called sphincters, control the flow of urine through the urethra. The inner sphincter works automatically, while the outer one is under your control. In males, the urethra is much longer and also carries semen, the fluid that contains sperm.
