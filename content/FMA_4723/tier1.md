Each brachiocephalic vein drains blood from the upper chest. This vein begins where the subclavian vein from the armpit meets the jugular veins from the head and neck. Veins draining the front of the chest wall also flow into each brachiocephalic vein. The right and left brachiocephalic veins join to form the superior vena cava, which returns blood to the heart.
