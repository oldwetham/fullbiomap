The epididymis is a coiled tube where sperm mature and are stored until ejaculation. As sperm pass through the epididymis, they gain the power to move on their own. The epididymis lies along the top and back of the testis, the organ that makes sperm. Straightened out, the tightly coiled epididymis would be about 6 meters (20 feet) long.
