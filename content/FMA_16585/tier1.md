Each hip bone is the attachment point for a lower limb. Each adult hip bone forms from three bones that fuse together in the late teenage years. These three parts meet to form the acetabulum, a deep cup that is part of the hip joint. The ischium, the rear and lower part of each hip bone, supports the body when you sit.
