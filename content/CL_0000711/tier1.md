A cumulus granulosa cell surrounds and nourishes the oocyte, or egg. Cumulus cells are a special type of granulosa cell, a supporting cell in the ovary. Around the fully grown egg, cumulus cells form the cumulus-oocyte complex. But other granulosa cells, at the wall of the follicle that holds the egg, do a different job.
