The nucleolus makes ribosomes, the cell parts that build proteins. The nucleolus is a dense area inside the nucleus, the cell's control center. The nucleolus builds ribosome pieces from proteins and RNA, a molecule involved in making proteins. These pieces then leave the nucleus through nuclear pores, small openings in its membrane.
