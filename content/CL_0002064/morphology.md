- **Nuclei:** 1  
  “have a basal nucleus” (Cell Ontology, CL:0002064 pancreatic acinar cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0002064)
- **Nucleus position:** basal  
  “have a basal nucleus” (Cell Ontology, CL:0002064 pancreatic acinar cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0002064)
- **Organelles:** ribosomes: 3 abundant  
  “the pancreas is responsible for creating several digestive enzymes and the cells that produce these enzymes contain many ribosomes” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 4.3 Eukaryotic Cells, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/4-3-eukaryotic-cells)
- **Special structure:** regular arrays of granular (rough) endoplasmic reticulum  
  “basophilic cytoplasm consisting of regular arrays of granular endoplasmic reticulum with mitochondria and dense secretory granules” (Cell Ontology, CL:0002064 pancreatic acinar cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0002064)
- **Special structure:** basophilic cytoplasm  
  “have a basal nucleus and basophilic cytoplasm” (Cell Ontology, CL:0002064 pancreatic acinar cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0002064)
- **Structure and job:** As a secretory cell of the pancreatic acini, it produces and releases digestive enzymes and mucins.  
  “A secretory cell found in pancreatic acini that secretes digestive enzymes and mucins.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0002064 pancreatic acinar cell, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0002064)
- **Structure and job:** Its many ribosomes suit its job of making large amounts of several digestive enzymes.  
  “Ribosomes are particularly abundant in cells that synthesize large amounts of protein. For example, the pancreas is responsible for creating several digestive enzymes and the cells that produce these enzymes contain many ribosomes.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 4.3 Eukaryotic Cells, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/4-3-eukaryotic-cells)
- **Structure and job:** It releases the digestive enzymes it makes from the cell by exocytosis.  
  “Cells of the stomach and pancreas produce and secrete digestive enzymes through exocytosis” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 3.1 The Cell Membrane, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/3-1-the-cell-membrane)
- **Structure and job:** When proteins and fats reach the duodenum, the hormone CCK signals the acini to secrete enzyme-rich pancreatic juice.  
  “The presence of proteins and fats in the duodenum stimulates the secretion of CCK, which then stimulates the acini to secrete enzyme-rich pancreatic juice and enhances the activity of secretin.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 23.6 Accessory Organs in Digestion: The Liver, Pancreas, and Gallbladder, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-6-accessory-organs-in-digestion-the-liver-pancreas-and-gallbladder)
- **Structure and job:** It makes trypsin, an enzyme that breaks proteins down into peptides.  
  “Pancreatic enzymes | Trypsin* | Pancreatic acinar cells | Proteins | Peptides” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 23.7 Chemical Digestion and Absorption: A Closer Look, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-7-chemical-digestion-and-absorption-a-closer-look)
- **Structure and job:** It makes pancreatic amylase, which breaks starches down into α-dextrins and small sugars such as maltose and maltotriose.  
  “Pancreatic enzymes | Pancreatic amylase | Pancreatic acinar cells | Polysaccharides (starches) | α-Dextrins, disaccharides (maltose), trisaccharides (maltotriose)” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 23.7 Chemical Digestion and Absorption: A Closer Look, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-7-chemical-digestion-and-absorption-a-closer-look)
- **Structure and job:** It makes pancreatic lipase, which splits triglycerides emulsified by bile salts into fatty acids and monoacylglycerides.  
  “Pancreatic enzymes | Pancreatic lipase | Pancreatic acinar cells | Triglycerides that have been emulsified by bile salts | Fatty acids and monoacylglycerides” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 23.7 Chemical Digestion and Absorption: A Closer Look, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-7-chemical-digestion-and-absorption-a-closer-look)
- **Structure and job:** It makes nucleases (ribonuclease and deoxyribonuclease) that break RNA and DNA down into nucleotides.  
  “Pancreatic enzymes | Nucleases | Pancreatic acinar cells | Ribonuclease: ribonucleic acids Deoxyribonuclease: deoxyribonucleic acids | Nucleotides” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 23.7 Chemical Digestion and Absorption: A Closer Look, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-7-chemical-digestion-and-absorption-a-closer-look)
