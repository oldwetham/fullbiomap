Each internal carotid artery carries oxygen-rich blood to the brain and eyes. This artery branches off a common carotid artery, which supplies the head and neck. The internal carotid artery reaches the brain through the carotid canal, a zig-zag tunnel through the base of the skull. The artery's branches then help form the circle of Willis, which keeps the brain's blood supply steady.
