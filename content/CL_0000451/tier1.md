Dendritic cells pick up antigens, foreign molecules that immune cells react to. Dendritic cells then carry the antigens to lymph nodes, small bean-shaped organs, to stimulate an immune response. Dendritic cells also engulf pathogens, agents that can cause disease. Dendritic cells live in the skin and in the lining of the nose, lungs, stomach and intestines.
