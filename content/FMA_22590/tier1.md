The popliteus bends the leg at the knee, a movement called flexion. To begin a knee bend, the popliteus makes a slight twist that unlocks the knee. The popliteus also steadies the back and outer side of the knee. Each popliteus forms the floor of the popliteal fossa, a diamond-shaped space behind the knee.
