Each lateral ventricle holds cerebrospinal fluid, a clear liquid that cushions the brain and spinal cord. The lateral ventricles are two hollow spaces, one deep inside each side of the brain. Inside each lateral ventricle, a spongy tissue called the choroid plexus makes this fluid. Each lateral ventricle opens into the third ventricle, letting the fluid pass through.
