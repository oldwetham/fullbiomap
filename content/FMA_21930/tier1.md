The external anal sphincter lets you choose when to pass feces, the waste product of digestion. This sphincter is a ring of muscle around the anus, outside the internal anal sphincter. The external anal sphincter is made of skeletal muscle, which you can control at will. Except when you pass feces, both sphincters usually stay closed.
