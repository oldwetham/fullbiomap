Each metacarpal bone is one of five bones that form the palm of the hand. The metacarpal bones lie between the wrist bones and the finger and thumb bones. Each metacarpal bone ends in a knuckle that joins a thumb or finger bone. The metacarpal bones are numbered 1 to 5, starting at the thumb.
