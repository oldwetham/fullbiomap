Merkel cells act as touch sensors, stimulating nerves whose signals the brain perceives as touch. Merkel cells also sense the slow vibrations made when a finger drags across a textured surface. These cells lie in the epidermis, the outermost layer of the skin. Merkel cells are especially common on the hands and feet.
