Of all the vertebrae, or spine bones, the lumbar vertebrae carry the most body weight. The five lumbar vertebrae, numbered L1 to L5, sit in the lower back. Each lumbar vertebra has a large, thick body, the front part that bears weight. The lumbar vertebrae also help form the back wall of the abdomen.
