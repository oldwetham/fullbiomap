The femoral artery gives off branches that bring blood to the deep muscles of the thigh. The femoral artery begins where the external iliac artery, the leg's main blood supply, crosses the body wall. The femoral artery lies in the femoral triangle, the area where the hip meets the leg. Behind the knee, the femoral artery takes a new name: the popliteal artery.
