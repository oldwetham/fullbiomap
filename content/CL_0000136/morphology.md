# Adipocyte: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Nucleus position:** peripheral  
  “The thin lines in the image are the cell membranes, and the nuclei are the small, black dots at the edges of the cells.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 33.2 Animal Primary Tissues, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/33-2-animal-primary-tissues)
- **Nucleus size:** small  
  “The thin lines in the image are the cell membranes, and the nuclei are the small, black dots at the edges of the cells.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 33.2 Animal Primary Tissues, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/33-2-animal-primary-tissues)
- **Organelles:** lipid droplets: 3 abundant  
  “Adipocytes are cells that store lipids as droplets that fill most of the cytoplasm.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 4.3 Connective Tissue Supports and Protects, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/4-3-connective-tissue-supports-and-protects)
- **Special structure:** stored triglycerides (fat)  
  “Adipose tissue is made up of cells called adipocytes that collect and store fat in the form of triglycerides, for energy metabolism.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 33.2 Animal Primary Tissues, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/33-2-animal-primary-tissues)
- **Structure and job:** Adipocytes are specialized fat stores: fat globules take up most of each cell's volume.  
  “Mammals store fats in specialized cells, or adipocytes, where fat globules occupy most of the cell’s volume.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 3.3 Lipids, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/3-3-lipids)
- **Structure and job:** By holding the body's surplus energy as fat, adipocytes act as an energy reserve and also help insulate the body.  
  “Adipose cells store surplus energy in the form of fat and contribute to the thermal insulation of the body.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 4.3 Connective Tissue Supports and Protects, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/4-3-connective-tissue-supports-and-protects)
- **Structure and job:** Adipocytes also make fat: lipogenesis, which builds lipids from acetyl CoA, takes place in their cytoplasm.  
  “This process, called lipogenesis, creates lipids (fat) from the acetyl CoA and takes place in the cytoplasm of adipocytes (fat cells) and hepatocytes (liver cells).” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 24.3 Lipid Metabolism, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/24-3-lipid-metabolism)
