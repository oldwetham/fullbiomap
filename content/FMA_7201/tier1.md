The large intestine finishes absorbing water and nutrients and forms feces, the semisolid waste of digestion. The large intestine frames the small intestine on three sides and ends at the anus. The large intestine has three parts: the cecum, a pouch at the start; the colon; and the rectum. The colon is home to many bacteria that help with digestion.
