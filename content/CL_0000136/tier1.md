An adipocyte is a fat cell whose main job is to store fat for later use. This fat is the body's store of extra energy, and adipocytes also help insulate the body. About half of the body's extra fat is stored in adipocytes under the skin. Inside each adipocyte, drops of fat fill most of the cell.
