Each ulnar artery carries blood to the forearm, wrist and hand. This artery begins where the brachial artery, the artery of the upper arm, splits into the radial and ulnar arteries. The ulnar artery runs along the ulna, the forearm bone on the pinky-finger side. At the wrist, the ulnar artery joins the radial artery to form the palmar arches, which supply blood to the hand.
