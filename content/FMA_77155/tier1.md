The popliteal artery carries blood from the femoral artery down behind the knee. An artery is a blood vessel that carries blood away from the heart. The popliteal artery runs through the popliteal fossa, a diamond-shaped space at the back of the knee. Then the popliteal artery splits into the anterior and posterior tibial arteries.
