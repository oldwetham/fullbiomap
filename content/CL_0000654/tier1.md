A primary oocyte goes through meiosis, a special type of cell division, to make an egg. Primary oocytes form in the ovary before birth, then stop partway through meiosis for years. Each primary oocyte and its supporting cells form a follicle. Later, the primary oocyte splits, and most of its material goes to one cell, the secondary oocyte.
