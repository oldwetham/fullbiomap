Inner hair cells are the main sensor cells for hearing, and they form a single row. This row lies in the cochlea, the hearing part of the inner ear. Each cell has stereocilia, hair-like projections that help detect sound waves. When the stereocilia bend, sound information travels along a nerve to the brain.
