The Golgi apparatus changes and packs new proteins for delivery inside or outside the cell. Cells that release a lot of material, such as salivary gland cells, have plenty of Golgi. The Golgi apparatus is a stack of flat discs made of membrane, like oddly shaped pancakes. Proteins arrive at one side in small sacs called vesicles and leave the other side in new vesicles.
