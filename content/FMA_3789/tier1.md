The abdominal aorta sends blood through its branches to the organs of the abdomen. This vessel is part of the aorta, the largest artery in the body. The abdominal aorta begins below the diaphragm, the dome-shaped muscle that separates the chest from the abdomen. This artery ends by splitting into the right and left common iliac arteries, which feed the pelvis and legs.
