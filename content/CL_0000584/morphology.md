# Enterocyte: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Shape:** column  
  “Other than in the anal canal, the mucosa of the colon is simple columnar epithelium made mostly of enterocytes (absorptive cells) and goblet cells.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 23.5 The Small and Large Intestines, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-5-the-small-and-large-intestines)
- **Surface:** microvilli  
  “An epithelial cell that has its apical plasma membrane folded into microvilli to provide ample surface for the absorption of nutrients from the intestinal lumen.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000584 enterocyte, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000584)
- **Surface:** brush border  
  “The micelles move into the brush border of the small intestine absorptive cells” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 34.3 Digestive System Processes, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/34-3-digestive-system-processes)
- **Special structure:** brush border enzymes (digestive enzymes set in the microvillus membrane)  
  “The small intestine’s absorptive cells also synthesize digestive enzymes and then place them in the plasma membranes of the microvilli.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 23.5 The Small and Large Intestines, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-5-the-small-and-large-intestines)
- **Special structure:** chylomicrons (protein-coated triglyceride globules)  
  “The long-chain fatty acids and monoglycerides recombine in the absorptive cells to form triglycerides, which aggregate into globules and become coated with proteins. These large spheres are called chylomicrons.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 34.3 Digestive System Processes, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/34-3-digestive-system-processes)
- **Structure and job:** Its apical membrane is folded into microvilli, which gives it a large surface for absorbing nutrients from the intestinal lumen.  
  “An epithelial cell that has its apical plasma membrane folded into microvilli to provide ample surface for the absorption of nutrients from the intestinal lumen.” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000584 enterocyte, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000584)
- **Structure and job:** It passes absorbed fat on as chylomicrons, which it releases by exocytosis into the lacteals of the villi.  
  “Chylomicrons leave the enterocytes by exocytosis and enter the lymphatic system via lacteals in the villi of the intestine.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 24.3 Lipid Metabolism, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/24-3-lipid-metabolism)
