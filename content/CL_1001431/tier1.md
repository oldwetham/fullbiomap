Principal cells help adjust how much water, sodium and potassium the body keeps. These cells line the collecting ducts, tubes in the kidney that carry urine. ADH, a hormone that tells the kidneys to take back water, makes principal cells add water channels. Another hormone, aldosterone, makes principal cells take back more sodium from the urine, and water follows the sodium.
