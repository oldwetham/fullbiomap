Each humerus, the single bone of the upper arm, helps form both the shoulder and elbow joints. The rounded head of the humerus meets the scapula, or shoulder blade, at the shoulder joint. The lower end of the humerus meets the ulna and radius, the forearm bones, at the elbow. Muscles that move the arm attach to the humerus.
