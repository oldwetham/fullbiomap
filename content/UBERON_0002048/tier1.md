Each lung lets the blood pick up oxygen and give off carbon dioxide, which we then breathe out. Each lung holds about 300 million alveoli, tiny grape-like sacs where the gases are swapped. The right lung is larger and has three lobes, or sections, while the smaller left lung has two.
