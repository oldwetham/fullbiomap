Purkinje cells spread each electrical signal to the muscle cells of the ventricles, the heart's lower pumping chambers. Purkinje cells are special heart muscle cells found just under the heart's inner lining. From the heart's bottom tip, these cells carry the signal up through the ventricle walls. So the ventricles contract from the bottom up, like squeezing a toothpaste tube from the bottom.
