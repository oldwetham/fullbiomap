Stereocilia let hair cells, the inner ear cells for hearing and balance, sense movement. Hair cells are named for their hair-like stereocilia, which are arranged from tallest to shortest. Sound waves make the stereocilia bend, and the bending sets off signals in the hair cells. For balance, hair cells with stereocilia sense the head's position and movement.
