Hematopoietic stem cells make red blood cells, white blood cells and platelets. These stem cells live in the bone marrow, the soft tissue that fills most bones. When one of these stem cells divides, one new cell stays a stem cell, so blood making can go on. Hematopoietic stem cells last through adult life, making new blood cells to replace old or used-up ones.
