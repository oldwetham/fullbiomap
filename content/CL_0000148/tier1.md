A melanocyte is a cell that makes melanin, the pigment that gives skin and hair their color. Melanin also helps shield living cells in the epidermis, the skin's outer layer, from ultraviolet (UV) rays. In the skin, melanocytes are scattered through the stratum basale, the deepest layer of the epidermis. Sunlight leads melanocytes to make more melanin, which darkens the skin into a tan.
