The manubrium forms joints with the clavicles, or collarbones, and the first ribs attach to it. It is the wide top part of the sternum, or breastbone. The manubrium's top edge has a shallow notch you can feel at the base of the neck. The manubrium meets the long middle part of the sternum at a slight bend.
