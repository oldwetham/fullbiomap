Dendrites receive signals from other neurons, or nerve cells, at contact points called synapses. Dendrites extend from the cell body, the main part of a neuron. Dendrites are usually short and branched, which gives them more surface area for receiving signals. From the dendrites, signals travel on to the cell body.
