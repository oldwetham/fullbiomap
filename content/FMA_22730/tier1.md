Each radial artery supplies blood to the forearm and wrist. Below the elbow, this artery branches off the brachial artery, the artery of the upper arm. The radial artery runs beside the radius, the forearm bone that gives this artery its name. You can feel the pulse, an artery's stretch and recoil, at the radial artery in the wrist.
