The plantaris belongs to a group of calf muscles that keep humans upright. Each plantaris runs at an angle between the gastrocnemius, the outermost calf muscle, and the deeper soleus. The calcaneal tendon, a fibrous cord, attaches each plantaris to the calcaneus, or heel bone. But dissections find no plantaris in about seven percent of bodies.
