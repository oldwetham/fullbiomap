Olfactory receptor cells are neurons (cells that receive and send signals) that detect chemicals so you can smell. Olfactory receptor cells sit in the olfactory epithelium, a patch of tissue high in the nose. Each cell has hair-like extensions called cilia that trap odor molecules. When the cell binds certain molecules you breathe in, it sends signals straight to the brain.
