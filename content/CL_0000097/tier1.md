A mast cell makes histamine and other molecules that promote inflammation, a basic immune response. Mature mast cells are found in all tissues except the bloodstream. A mast cell holds histamine in many granules, small sacs wrapped in a membrane. When irritated, a mast cell releases histamine, which widens blood vessels and increases blood flow.
