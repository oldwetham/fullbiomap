# Osteoblast: morphology profile (unreviewed)

What Bio Map draws for this cell type, and the source sentence behind each choice. Generated from the front matter; edit the front matter, not this list.

- **Structure and job:** Osteoblasts build new bone and are found where bone is growing, including the periosteum and endosteum.  
  “The osteoblast is the bone cell responsible for forming new bone and is found in the growing portions of bone, including the periosteum and endosteum.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.3 Bone Structure, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-3-bone-structure)
- **Structure and job:** Osteoblasts do not divide; they make and secrete bone's collagen matrix and calcium salts.  
  “Osteoblasts, which do not divide, synthesize and secrete the collagen matrix and calcium salts.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.3 Bone Structure, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-3-bone-structure)
- **Structure and job:** Once the matrix it secreted calcifies around it, the trapped osteoblast changes its structure and becomes an osteocyte, the main cell of mature bone.  
  “As the secreted matrix surrounding the osteoblast calcifies, the osteoblast becomes trapped within it; as a result, it changes in structure and becomes an osteocyte, the primary cell of mature bone and the most common type of bone cell.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.3 Bone Structure, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-3-bone-structure)
- **Structure and job:** As a skeleton-forming cell, the osteoblast secretes osteoid and can produce hydroxyapatite-mineralized matrix.  
  “Skeletogenic cell that secretes osteoid, is capable of producing mineralized (hydroxyapatite) matrix” (Cell Ontology, CL:0000062 osteoblast, http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/CL_0000062)
- **Structure and job:** Working at the bone surface, osteoblasts secrete matrix there, adding bone that widens the bone (appositional growth).  
  “Appositional growth is the increase in the diameter of bones by the addition of bony tissue at the surface of bones. Osteoblasts at the bone surface secrete bone matrix, and osteoclasts on the inner surface break down bone.” (OpenStax Biology 2e, 38.2 Bone, https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/38-2-bone)
- **Structure and job:** Osteoblasts continually form new bone while osteoclasts break old bone down, and the balance between the two constantly but subtly reshapes bone.  
  “Osteoclasts are continually breaking down old bone while osteoblasts are continually forming new bone. The ongoing balance between osteoblasts and osteoclasts is responsible for the constant but subtle reshaping of bone.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 6.3 Bone Structure, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/6-3-bone-structure)
- **Structure and job:** Osteoblasts remove calcium from the blood and build it into the bony matrix, and calcitonin from the thyroid increases this activity.  
  “Calcitonin is released from the thyroid gland in response to elevated blood levels of calcium. The hormone increases the activity of osteoblasts, which remove calcium from the blood and incorporate calcium into the bony matrix.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 26.3 Electrolyte Balance, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/26-3-electrolyte-balance)
- **Structure and job:** Osteoblasts also make osteocalcin, which stimulates pancreatic beta cells to increase insulin production.  
  “Osteocalcin, produced by osteoblasts, stimulates the pancreatic beta cells to increase insulin production.” (OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e, 17.10 Organs with Secondary Endocrine Functions, https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/17-10-organs-with-secondary-endocrine-functions)
