The fibularis tertius belongs to the front group of lower-leg muscles that move the foot and toes. This small muscle starts on the front of the fibula, the thin outer bone of the leg. The fibularis tertius is linked to the extensor digitorum longus, which helps lift the front of the foot. The fibularis tertius is sometimes fused to that muscle and is missing in some people.
