Each pronator quadratus turns the forearm so the palm faces backward, a movement called pronation. The pronator teres is the other muscle that does this. The pronator quadratus runs from the ulna to the radius, the bones of the forearm. With the palm turned backward, the radius and ulna cross in an X shape.
